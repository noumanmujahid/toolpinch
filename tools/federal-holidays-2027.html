The short answer

How many federal holidays are in 2027?

There are 11 federal holidays in 2027, and federal employees get an extra day off on Friday, December 31, 2027, when New Year's Day 2028 (a Saturday) is observed. Three 2027 holidays move because they fall on a weekend: Juneteenth is observed Friday, June 18; Independence Day Monday, July 5; and Christmas Day Friday, December 24.

When a holiday falls on a Saturday, federal employees get the Friday before off, but Federal Reserve Banks stay open that Friday, so most banks are open on June 18, December 24 and December 31, 2027. When a holiday falls on a Sunday, everyone observes the Monday after.

Three-day weekends in 2027

  • New Year's Day: Fri, Jan 1 to Sun, Jan 3
  • Martin Luther King Jr. Day: Fri, Jan 15 to Mon, Jan 18
  • Washington's Birthday: Fri, Feb 12 to Mon, Feb 15
  • Memorial Day: Fri, May 28 to Mon, May 31
  • Juneteenth: Fri, Jun 18 to Sun, Jun 20
  • Independence Day: Fri, Jul 2 to Mon, Jul 5
  • Labor Day: Fri, Sep 3 to Mon, Sep 6
  • Columbus Day: Fri, Oct 8 to Mon, Oct 11
  • Christmas Day: Fri, Dec 24 to Sun, Dec 26
  • New Year's Day: Fri, Dec 31 to Sun, Jan 2, 2028

See long weekends 2027 for ways to turn these into longer breaks with a day or two of leave.

What's closed on federal holidays

  • Federal offices and the Postal Service are closed and there is no regular mail delivery on all 11 holidays.
  • Banks generally follow the Federal Reserve schedule above.
  • Stock markets follow their own calendar: the NYSE and Nasdaq stay open on Columbus Day and Veterans Day, and close on Good Friday (March 26, 2027), which is not a federal holiday.
  • Private employers choose which holidays they observe. Federal law doesn't require private employers to give holidays off or pay a premium; see the holiday pay calculator.

Sources: OPM federal holidays, Federal Reserve holidays observed, 5 U.S.C. 6103.

Frequently asked questions

What are the federal holidays in 2027?

New Year's Day (Jan 1), Martin Luther King Jr. Day (Jan 18), Washington's Birthday (Feb 15), Memorial Day (May 31), Juneteenth (observed Fri, Jun 18), Independence Day (observed Mon, Jul 5), Labor Day (Sep 6), Columbus Day (Oct 11), Veterans Day (Thu, Nov 11), Thanksgiving (Nov 25) and Christmas Day (observed Fri, Dec 24). New Year's Day 2028 is observed Fri, Dec 31, 2027.

Is Christmas Eve 2027 a federal holiday?

Friday, December 24, 2027 is the observed Christmas Day holiday for federal employees, because December 25 falls on a Saturday.

Are banks open on December 24, 2027?

Generally yes. Federal Reserve Banks are open on the Friday before a holiday that falls on a Saturday, so most banks are open on December 24 and December 31, 2027, even though federal offices are closed.

When is the July 4th holiday in 2027?

Independence Day falls on Sunday, July 4, 2027, so the federal holiday is Monday, July 5.

How many paid holidays do federal employees get in 2027?

Eleven federal holidays fall in calendar 2027, plus Friday, December 31, 2027 for New Year's Day 2028, so federal employees have 12 holiday days off during calendar year 2027.

Is Good Friday a federal holiday?

No. Good Friday (March 26, 2027) is not a federal holiday, though the stock markets close.
