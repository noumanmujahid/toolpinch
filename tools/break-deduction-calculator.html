How to use the break deduction calculator

Use this focused calculator when you only need to remove unpaid time from one shift. Combine lunch and other unpaid breaks into one minute total, then compare paid hours in standard and decimal formats.

Formula

Paid time = total shift duration − unpaid lunch and break minutes.

Worked examples

Thirty-minute lunch

A shift from 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM lasts 8.5 hours. Subtract 30 minutes to get 8 paid hours.

Two unpaid breaks

A 30-minute lunch plus a 15-minute unpaid break equals 45 minutes to deduct.

Overnight example

From 11:00 PM to 7:30 AM is 8.5 hours; subtracting 30 minutes leaves 8 paid hours.

Frequently asked questions

Should I include paid breaks?

No. Deduct only time your employer treats as unpaid.

Can I add more than one break?

Yes. Add all unpaid break minutes and enter the combined total.

Does it support overnight shifts?

Yes. An earlier end time is interpreted as the following day.

Can I use the result for payroll?

Use it as a check, then follow your employer’s rounding and payroll policies.

Assumptions and limitations

Results depend on the values entered and are educational estimates. Pay figures exclude taxes, deductions and benefits. Workplace rules, rounding practices and legal requirements vary by location and employer.
