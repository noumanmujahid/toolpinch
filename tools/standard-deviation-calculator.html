The short answer

How it works

Standard deviation measures how far values typically sit from the mean. Find the mean, square each value's distance from it, average those squares and take the square root. For 2, 4, 4, 4, 5, 5, 7, 9 the mean is 5 and the population standard deviation is 2; the sample standard deviation is 2.14.

Population or sample?

Use the population formula (divide by n) when your data includes every member of the group you care about, such as the scores of everyone in one class. Use the sample formula (divide by n − 1) when your data is a sample used to estimate a larger group, such as 50 customers surveyed out of thousands. Dividing by n − 1 (Bessel's correction) corrects the tendency of a sample to understate the spread.

Reading the result

For roughly bell-shaped data, about 68% of values fall within 1 standard deviation of the mean, 95% within 2 and 99.7% within 3. If exam scores average 70 with a standard deviation of 8, about two-thirds of students scored between 62 and 78.

A small standard deviation means values cluster tightly around the mean; a large one means they are spread out. Because it is in the same units as the data, it is easier to interpret than the variance. To compare spread between datasets with different means, use the coefficient of variation (standard deviation ÷ mean).

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate standard deviation?

Find the mean, subtract it from each value, square the results, add them up, divide by n (population) or n − 1 (sample), and take the square root.

What is the difference between population and sample standard deviation?

Population standard deviation divides by the number of values n and is used when you have every value. Sample standard deviation divides by n − 1 and is used to estimate the spread of a larger group from a sample.

What does a standard deviation of 0 mean?

Every value is identical, so there is no spread around the mean.

What is the 68-95-99.7 rule?

For normally distributed data, about 68% of values lie within one standard deviation of the mean, 95% within two and 99.7% within three.

What is variance?

Variance is the average squared distance from the mean, and standard deviation is its square root. Standard deviation is easier to read because it uses the same units as the data.

Which one does Excel use?

STDEV.S (or STDEV) gives the sample standard deviation; STDEV.P gives the population standard deviation.
