How to use the what time will it be calculator

Answer questions such as “What time will it be 8 hours from now?” using the current time and timezone on your device.

Formula or method

Target time = current local time + entered minutes, hours or days.

Worked examples

Eight hours from morning

If it is 9:00 AM now, eight hours from now is 5:00 PM today.

Crossing midnight

Ninety minutes after 11:30 PM is 1:00 AM tomorrow, so the result also changes the date.

A full day from now

Twenty-four hours from now is the same local clock time on the next day, except around daylight-saving changes.

Frequently asked questions

Which timezone does the answer use?

It uses the timezone reported by your browser and device, so visitors receive a local answer wherever they are.

What happens when the result crosses midnight?

The calculator shows both the target time and the new calendar date.

Can I enter decimal hours?

Yes. For example, 1.5 hours means one hour and 30 minutes.

Can daylight saving change a 24-hour result?

Yes. Adding an exact 24-hour duration across a daylight-saving transition can produce a different wall-clock hour in regions that change clocks.

Limitations

The result depends on your device clock and timezone settings.
