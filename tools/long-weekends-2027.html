The short answer

How many long weekends are in 2027?

10 three-day weekends on the US federal holiday calendar, with no vacation days needed. The longest break of the year: take December 27 to 31 off (5 PTO days) and get 10 days in a row (Fri, Dec 24 – Sun, Jan 2, 2028). If your employer gives December 31 off, as the federal government does, it takes only 4 PTO days.

2027 is a good year for time off. Five holidays land on a Monday and three on a Friday, and Christmas and New Year's Day 2028 both fall on Saturdays, which pushes their days off onto the Fridays before. Use the planner above to see the best breaks for the holidays your employer actually gives you.

Two holidays fall on a Thursday in 2027: Veterans Day (November 11) and Thanksgiving (November 25). Taking the Friday after each turns them into four-day weekends for one PTO day. With 4 PTO days around Thanksgiving you get 9 days off (Sat, Nov 20 – Sun, Nov 28).

How to get the most from your PTO in 2027

Book the bridges early

The best breaks, around Thanksgiving, Christmas and July 4, are the ones your coworkers want too. Request them as soon as your employer opens 2027 PTO requests.

Check your real holiday list

Federal holidays aren't required for private employers. Many observe 6 to 10, often including the day after Thanksgiving and sometimes Good Friday (March 26, 2027). Tick yours in the planner.

Mind the carry-over rules

If unused PTO expires at year end, plan a December break. If it carries over, saving 4 or 5 days for the 10-day Christmas to New Year break is one of the best deals of the year.

Assumptions and limitations

Breaks assume a Monday to Friday schedule with weekends off. The planner picks breaks with the most days off per PTO day and avoids overlapping breaks. Your employer's holiday calendar, blackout dates and approval rules decide what you can actually take.

Frequently asked questions

How many long weekends are in 2027?

10 three-day weekends on the US federal holiday calendar: New Year's Day, MLK Day, Presidents Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Columbus Day, Christmas, and the New Year's Day 2028 weekend for federal employees.

When is the best time to take vacation in 2027?

Christmas to New Year. Take December 27 to 31 off (5 PTO days) and you get 10 days in a row, from Friday, December 24 to Sunday, January 2, 2028. If December 31 is a holiday for you, as it is for federal employees, you need only 4 PTO days.

Which 2027 holidays fall on a Friday?

New Year's Day (January 1), Juneteenth (observed Friday, June 18), Christmas (observed Friday, December 24) and New Year's Day 2028 (observed Friday, December 31 for federal employees).

Is July 4, 2027 a long weekend?

Yes. July 4 falls on a Sunday, so the holiday is observed on Monday, July 5, giving a three-day weekend from Saturday, July 3.

How do I get 9 days off with 4 PTO days in 2027?

Pick any Monday holiday, such as Memorial Day, Labor Day or Presidents Day, and take the Tuesday to Friday after it. With the weekend before, that is 9 days in a row.

Is Good Friday 2027 a holiday?

Good Friday is March 26, 2027. It isn't a federal holiday, but some employers, schools and the stock markets close. Tick it in the planner if your employer gives it.

Is the day after Thanksgiving 2027 a holiday?

Friday, November 26, 2027 isn't a federal holiday, but many private employers give it off. If yours does, Thanksgiving becomes a four-day weekend with no PTO.

Can I add my planned breaks to my calendar?

Yes. After running the planner, choose "Add breaks to my calendar" to download an .ics file for Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar.
