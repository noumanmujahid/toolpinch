What Is Your Weighted Grade So Far?

Combine assignment scores using their percentage weights.

The short answer

How it works

Multiply each score by its weight, add the results and divide by the total weight you entered. Homework 92% (weight 20), quizzes 78% (weight 30) and a midterm 85% (weight 25) give (18.4 + 23.4 + 21.25) ÷ 75 × 100 = 84.1% so far.

Worked example

CategoryScoreWeightScore × weight
Homework92%20%18.40
Quizzes78%30%23.40
Midterm85%25%21.25
Total75%63.05

63.05 ÷ 75 = 84.1%. The remaining 25% (perhaps a final exam) has not been graded yet; dividing by the weight completed gives your standing on the work done so far.

Many schools add plus and minus grades and some use stricter cut-offs (for example 93% for an A). Your syllabus is the authority.

Weighted versus unweighted averages

A simple average treats every score equally: (92 + 78 + 85) ÷ 3 = 85%. The weighted average is 84.1% because the quiz score, the lowest, carries the most weight. When categories have different weights, always use the weighted method. If your course grades by total points instead of weights, divide the points earned by the points possible.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a weighted grade?

Multiply each score by its weight, add the products and divide by the sum of the weights. Scores of 92, 78 and 85 with weights 20, 30 and 25 give 84.1%.

What if my weights don't add up to 100%?

That is normal partway through a course. Dividing by the weight completed so far gives your current standing on the work graded.

What is the difference between weighted and points-based grading?

Weighted grading assigns each category a fixed share of the grade. Points-based grading adds all points earned and divides by all points possible, so larger assignments count more automatically.

What percentage is a B?

On the common US scale, 80% to 89%. Schools that use plus and minus grades often set B− at 80–82, B at 83–86 and B+ at 87–89.

How do I calculate my grade if a category is empty?

Leave it out and divide by the weights of the categories that have scores. Including it as zero would understate your grade.

How do I find what I need on the final exam?

Use the final grade calculator, which works backwards from your current weighted grade and the final's weight.
