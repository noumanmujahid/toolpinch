Where the 2027 raise stands

Will federal employees get a raise in 2027?

As of November 27, 2026, the 2027 GS raise is proposed at 0%. In late August 2026 the President sent Congress an alternative pay plan with no across-the-board increase and no locality increase for 2027, and a 3.8% raise for federal law enforcement through special pay rates. Congress can still change this in a funding bill. Current funding runs out on December 11, 2026.

If the freeze holds, the 2027 GS pay scale will be the same as 2026. A GS-12 step 1 in the Washington, DC area would stay at $102,415, and a GS-9 step 1 in the Rest of U.S. area at $61,722.

2027 federal pay raise timeline

  • Late August 2026: the President's alternative pay plan sets the 2027 adjustment at 0% for most civilian employees, with 3.8% for law enforcement.
  • November 2026: 40 federal unions asked appropriators for at least 3.8%. The FAIR Act would give an average 4.1% (3.1% base plus 1% locality). Neither the House nor the Senate spending bill included a general raise as of late November.
  • December 11, 2026: current government funding expires. A raise could be added to the next spending bill.
  • Mid to late December 2026: the President's executive order sets the final 2027 pay tables, and OPM publishes them.
  • January 10, 2027: new rates take effect for most employees, at the start of the first pay period of 2027 (paid by NFC on February 1). A raise passed later can be made retroactive.

How GS pay works

  • Grade and step: your grade (GS-1 to GS-15) sets the pay range; steps 1 to 10 are within-grade increases. Steps 2 to 4 come after 52 weeks each, steps 5 to 7 after 104 weeks, and steps 8 to 10 after 156 weeks, with acceptable performance.
  • Locality pay: a percentage added to base pay for your duty station, from 17.06% (Rest of U.S.) to 46.34% (San Jose-San Francisco-Oakland).
  • Pay cap: GS pay including locality can't exceed Executive Schedule level IV, $197,200 in 2026.
  • Hourly and biweekly pay: divide the annual salary by 2,087 hours for the hourly rate; biweekly pay is 80 hours at that rate.

Sources: OPM Salary Table 2026-GS and 2026 locality tables, OPM notice on January 2026 pay adjustments, Government Executive on the 2027 pay plan and FedSmith on the December funding bill.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a federal pay raise in 2027?

Not under the current plan. The President's alternative pay plan sets the 2027 raise at 0% for most GS employees, with 3.8% for law enforcement. Congress could add a raise in a spending bill before the final pay tables are set in December 2026.

What is the GS pay scale for 2027?

If the proposed freeze takes effect, the 2027 GS pay scale is the same as 2026: GS-1 step 1 base pay of $22,584 up to GS-15 step 10 base pay of $164,301, plus locality pay of 17.06% to 46.34%, capped at $197,200.

When will the 2027 GS pay tables be released?

Usually in late December, after the President signs the pay executive order. New rates take effect January 10, 2027, the first pay period of the year.

How much is a GS-12 step 1 in 2026?

$76,463 base pay. With locality pay it is $89,508 in the Rest of U.S. area and $102,415 in the Washington, DC area.

Do law enforcement officers get a raise in 2027?

Yes, under the President's plan federal law enforcement personnel receive a 3.8% basic pay increase in 2027 through special pay rates.

How do I calculate my hourly GS pay?

Divide your annual salary, including locality pay, by 2,087 hours. Biweekly gross pay is that hourly rate times 80.
