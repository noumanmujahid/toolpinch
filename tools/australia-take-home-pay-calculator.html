The short answer

How much tax do you pay in Australia in 2026–27?

Residents pay no tax on the first $18,200, then 15% to $45,000, 30% to $135,000, 37% to $190,000 and 45% above, plus the 2% Medicare levy. On an $80,000 salary you pay $14,520 tax and $1,600 Medicare levy and take home $63,880 a year ($2,457 a fortnight).

The lowest rate falls from 16% to 15% on 1 July 2026, the first step of the tax cut legislated in 2025, and to 14% from 1 July 2027.

Low income tax offset (LITO): up to $700 for taxable income up to $37,500, reducing to nothing at $66,667. With LITO, a resident pays no income tax on about the first $22,866.

Medicare levy: 2% of taxable income. Singles earning $28,011 or less pay nothing, with a phase-in up to $35,013. A Medicare levy surcharge of 1% to 1.5% applies to higher earners without private hospital cover (not included).

How the calculator works

Taxable income is your salary minus salary sacrifice to super. Super guarantee is paid by your employer on top of your salary at 12% and is not deducted from your take-home pay. The calculator assumes you are an Australian resident for tax purposes for the whole year with no other income, deductions or offsets. Working holiday makers and foreign residents pay different rates.

Frequently asked questions

How much is $80,000 after tax in Australia?

$63,880 a year in 2026–27, or $5,323 a month and $2,457 a fortnight, after $14,520 income tax and $1,600 Medicare levy.

How much is $100,000 after tax in Australia?

$77,480 a year, or $2,980 a fortnight, with no HELP debt.

How much is $60,000 after tax in Australia?

$50,380 a year, or $1,938 a fortnight.

What are the tax rates for 2026–27?

Nil up to $18,200, 15% to $45,000, 30% to $135,000, 37% to $190,000 and 45% above, plus the 2% Medicare levy.

What is the tax-free threshold?

$18,200. With the low income tax offset, residents pay no income tax on about the first $22,866 of taxable income.

What is the super guarantee rate for 2026–27?

12% of ordinary time earnings, paid by your employer on top of your salary, up to a maximum contribution base of $270,830 a year.

When do I start repaying HELP?

When your repayment income is above $69,528 in 2026–27. You repay 15 cents for each dollar above that, rising to 17 cents above $129,717.

When does the 2026–27 financial year run?

From 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027.
