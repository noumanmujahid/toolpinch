The short answer

How many paydays are in 2027?

Biweekly: 26 or 27, depending on your schedule and holiday rules. Weekly: 52 or 53. Twice a month: 24. Monthly: 12.

The reason 2027 is unusual: it starts on a Friday. A year is 365 days, which is 52 weeks plus one day, so 2027 has 53 Fridays. If your biweekly payday falls on Friday, January 1, 2027, the schedule lands on Friday, December 31, 2027 too. That is 27 scheduled paydays in one calendar year instead of the usual 26.

There is a catch. January 1 is New Year's Day, and banks are closed. Most payroll providers pay the business day before a bank holiday, which moves that first check to Thursday, December 31, 2026. In that case 2027 ends up with 26 paydays, and the extra one shows up in 2026 instead. Use the calculator above to see which applies to you.

How to find out which schedule you're on

  1. Find one real payday. Look at a recent pay stub or bank deposit. Any date works, even one from 2026.
  2. Check how often you're paid. Paid every other Friday is biweekly. Paid on the 15th and the last day of the month is semimonthly.
  3. Enter it above. The calculator extends your pattern through all of 2027, so you don't need to know whether you're on "schedule A" or "schedule B".
  4. Save it. Use "Add to my calendar" to put every payday in Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar, or "Download CSV" for a spreadsheet.

Quick check for biweekly Friday pay: if you were paid on Friday, December 18, 2026, you're on the schedule that can produce 27 paydays. If you were paid on Friday, December 24 or 25, 2026, you're on the other schedule, with 26.

2027 weekly paydays

With weekly Friday pay, 2027 has 53 Fridays. If the January 1 check is paid on December 31, 2026, you get 52 paydays in 2027. Months with five Friday paydays: April, July, October and December.

What a 27-payday year means for employers

Salaried employees are often paid their annual salary divided by 26. In a year with 27 paydays, that formula pays out 27/26 of the salary, which is about 3.8% more than budgeted. For a $65,000 salary, that is an extra $2,500.

Option 1: pay the extra check

Keep each paycheck the same and budget for one extra payroll in 2027. Employees like it, but it raises payroll costs for the year.

Option 2: divide by 27

Pay salary ÷ 27 per check so the annual total stays the same. Each check shrinks. For $65,000 that is $2,407.41 instead of $2,500.00. Employees should be told in advance.

Option 3: shift the payday

Paying the January 1 check on December 31, 2026 keeps 2027 at 26 paydays. It moves the extra check into 2026 for tax reporting, so it shows up on that year's W-2.

Hourly employees are paid for the hours they work, so an extra payday doesn't add cost. It only changes which year the pay is reported in.

When is the next year with 27 biweekly paydays?

For Friday biweekly payrolls, 27-payday years come around every 5 to 6 years, alternating between the two Friday schedules: 2027, then 2032, 2038 and 2044. Each of these years has 53 Fridays. Whether your own payroll gets the 27th check depends on which of the two Friday schedules it follows and how it handles paydays that land on bank holidays. If you're on the other schedule, 2032 is likely your 27-payday year.

How to use three-paycheck months

If most of your bills are monthly, budget as if you get two biweekly checks per month. The months with a third check then become breathing room: an emergency fund top-up, an extra debt payment, or annual bills like insurance premiums and car registration. Weekly earners can do the same with their five-payday months.

Assumptions and limitations

Bank holidays follow the Federal Reserve schedule: banks close on Monday when a holiday falls on Sunday, and stay open on Friday when a holiday falls on Saturday. Your employer's payroll policy decides whether a payday moves earlier, later or not at all. Direct deposit timing also depends on your bank. Check your pay stub or HR team for your exact dates.

Frequently asked questions

How many biweekly paydays are there in 2027?

Either 26 or 27. A Friday biweekly schedule starting January 1, 2027 has 27 scheduled paydays, but most employers pay the January 1 check on December 31, 2026 because banks are closed for New Year's Day, which leaves 26 in 2027. A schedule starting January 8 has 26.

Which months have three paychecks in 2027?

For biweekly Friday pay starting January 8: April and October. For the schedule starting January 1: July and December, plus January if the New Year's Day check isn't moved into 2026.

What happens when payday falls on a holiday in 2027?

Most employers pay the business day before. Banks close on New Year's Day (Jan 1), Martin Luther King Jr. Day (Jan 18), Presidents Day (Feb 15), Memorial Day (May 31), Independence Day observed (Jul 5), Labor Day (Sep 6), Columbus Day (Oct 11), Veterans Day (Nov 11) and Thanksgiving (Nov 25).

Are banks open on June 18 and December 24, 2027?

Yes. Juneteenth (June 19) and Christmas (December 25) fall on Saturdays in 2027. Federal employees get the Friday before off, but Federal Reserve Banks stay open on those Fridays, so direct deposits normally arrive as usual.

Is 2027 a 53-week payroll year?

For weekly Friday payrolls, yes: 2027 has 53 Fridays. Whether all 53 paydays land in 2027 depends on whether your employer moves the January 1 payday into December 2026.

What is the difference between biweekly and semimonthly pay?

Biweekly means every two weeks, which gives 26 paydays in most years. Semimonthly means twice a month on fixed dates, which always gives 24. Biweekly checks are smaller, but there are two more of them each year.

How do I add my 2027 paydays to my calendar?

Enter your schedule in the calculator, then choose "Add to my calendar (.ics)". The file opens in Google Calendar, Outlook and Apple Calendar and adds every payday as an all-day event.

When is the next year with 27 biweekly paydays?

For Friday biweekly payrolls, 2027 is followed by 2032, 2038 and 2044. Which of those years affects you depends on which Friday schedule your employer uses.

Does a 27th paycheck mean I pay more tax?

Your tax rates don't change. If your employer pays salary ÷ 26 on all 27 checks, you earn more in 2027, so total tax owed rises with that income. If they pay salary ÷ 27, your annual pay and tax stay about the same, but each check is smaller.

How much smaller is each check when salary is divided by 27?

About 3.7% smaller. On a $60,000 salary, each check drops from $2,307.69 (÷26) to $2,222.22 (÷27), a difference of $85.47.

How do I know if I'm on the 27-payday schedule?

If you're paid every other Friday and one of your paydays was Friday, December 18, 2026, your schedule includes January 1 and December 31, 2027. Enter any real payday in the calculator to check.

Is the Christmas Eve 2027 payday moved?

Usually not. Christmas Day 2027 falls on a Saturday. Federal employees get Friday, December 24 off, but Federal Reserve Banks stay open that day, so direct deposits normally arrive as scheduled.
