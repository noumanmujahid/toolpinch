Understand the estimate

How to calculate overtime pay

Use this calculator when you already know which hours qualify as overtime and the multiplier that applies. It separates regular earnings from overtime earnings and shows one gross total before taxes or deductions.

Overtime pay formula

Regular pay = regular hours × hourly rate. Overtime pay = overtime hours × hourly rate × multiplier. Add both amounts for estimated gross pay.

Worked example

At $25 per hour, 40 regular hours produce $1,000. Five overtime hours at 1.5× produce $187.50, for estimated gross pay of $1,187.50.

Which multiplier should I use?

Use 1.5 for time-and-a-half, 2 for double time, or the multiplier supplied by your employer or applicable rule. The calculator does not decide which hours legally qualify.

Jurisdiction limitations

Overtime thresholds and exemptions vary by country, state, award, contract and occupation. Confirm current rules with an official labor authority or qualified adviser before relying on the estimate.

Overtime calculator FAQs

Does this calculate taxes?

No. Results are gross estimates before taxes, benefits and payroll deductions.

Can I calculate double time?

Yes. Select the 2× multiplier and enter only the hours paid at that rate.

Does overtime always begin after 40 hours?

No. Rules vary by location, employment status and agreement. Enter the hours you have confirmed qualify.

Can salaried workers receive overtime?

Eligibility depends on local law and the worker's duties and classification. This calculator does not determine eligibility.
