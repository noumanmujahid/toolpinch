How to use the overtime pay calculator

Use this calculator after confirming which hours qualify for overtime and which multiplier applies. It separates ordinary earnings from premium overtime earnings so the estimate is easy to verify.

Formula

Regular pay = regular hours × hourly rate. Overtime pay = overtime hours × hourly rate × multiplier. Total gross pay is the sum.

Worked examples

Five hours at time-and-a-half

At $25 per hour, 40 regular hours equal $1,000 and five overtime hours at 1.5× equal $187.50. Total gross pay is $1,187.50.

Four hours of double time

At $20 per hour, four hours at 2× add $160 in overtime pay.

Mixed overtime rates

Calculate time-and-a-half and double-time hours separately when both rates apply, then add the results.

Frequently asked questions

Does overtime always start after 40 hours?

No. Thresholds vary by jurisdiction, occupation, contract and employee classification.

Can this calculate double time?

Yes. Select the 2× multiplier and enter only the hours paid at that rate.

Are taxes included?

No. Results are gross estimates before taxes, benefits and deductions.

Does this determine legal eligibility?

No. Confirm eligibility and current rules with an official labor authority or qualified adviser.

Assumptions and limitations

Results depend on the values entered and are educational estimates. Pay figures exclude taxes, deductions and benefits. Workplace rules, rounding practices and legal requirements vary by location and employer.
