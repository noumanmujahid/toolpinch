Which Prime Numbers Make Up This Number?

Break a positive whole number into prime factors.

Prime Factor Calculator

Enter values to see one clear answer.

Method

Repeatedly divide the whole number by the smallest possible prime until the remaining value is prime.

Example

360 = 2 × 2 × 2 × 3 × 3 × 5.
