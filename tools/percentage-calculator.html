Method

Choose the relationship first. Percentage of a number uses P ÷ 100 × value. What-percent uses part ÷ whole × 100. Percentage change uses (new − original) ÷ |original| × 100.

Example

20% of 150 is 30 because 0.20 × 150 = 30.
