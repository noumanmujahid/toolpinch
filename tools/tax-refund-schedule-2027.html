The short answer

When will I get my tax refund in 2027?

If you e-file and choose direct deposit, most refunds arrive within 21 days of the IRS accepting your return. File on February 1, 2027 and you can expect it by about February 22. If you claim the Earned Income Credit or Additional Child Tax Credit, the IRS can't send your refund before mid-February; in 2026 most of those refunds arrived by March 2.

The IRS hasn't announced the first day of the 2027 filing season yet. It was January 26 in 2026, so this page assumes Monday, January 25, 2027; change it in the calculator once the date is official. The filing deadline is Thursday, April 15, 2027.

How to get your refund faster

  • E-file instead of mailing a paper return.
  • Choose direct deposit. The IRS is phasing out paper refund checks.
  • Double-check your Social Security number, bank routing and account numbers, and income from every W-2 and 1099. Mismatches are the most common reason a refund is held.
  • Respond quickly if the IRS asks you to verify your identity.

Track your refund with the IRS Where's My Refund tool, which updates once a day, usually overnight, and moves through three stages: return received, refund approved and refund sent. To estimate how much you'll get back, use the tax refund estimator.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to get a tax refund in 2027?

Most refunds for e-filed returns are issued within 21 days of the IRS accepting the return. Paper returns take much longer, and refunds with the Earned Income Credit or Additional Child Tax Credit can't be sent before mid-February.

When does the IRS start accepting tax returns in 2027?

The IRS hasn't announced it yet; it usually opens in late January. The 2026 season opened January 26, 2026.

When will I get my refund if I claim the child tax credit?

If you claim the Additional Child Tax Credit (the refundable part) or the Earned Income Credit, the IRS can't issue your refund before mid-February. In 2026 most early e-filers with direct deposit received these refunds by March 2.

How often does Where's My Refund update?

Once a day, usually overnight. It shows status about 24 hours after an e-filed return is accepted, or about 4 weeks after a paper return is mailed.

How long does an amended return refund take?

Amended returns (Form 1040-X) are processed separately and typically take 8 to 12 weeks, sometimes up to 16 weeks.

What is the tax filing deadline in 2027?

Thursday, April 15, 2027, for 2026 federal returns. An extension moves the filing deadline to October 15, 2027, but not the deadline to pay.
