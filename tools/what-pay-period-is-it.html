How it works

What pay period is it today?

The calculator shows today's federal pay period as soon as the page loads. Federal pay periods are 14 days long, run Sunday to Saturday, and are numbered 1 to 26 from the start of each leave year.

For example: Dec 1, 2026 is pay period 24 of leave year 2026 (day 3 of 14), paid by NFC on Dec 21; Mar 15, 2027 is pay period 5 of leave year 2027 (day 9 of 14), paid by NFC on Mar 29; Jul 4, 2027 is pay period 13 of leave year 2027 (day 8 of 14), paid by NFC on Jul 19.

Each leave year starts with the first full biweekly pay period of the calendar year, so it begins in early January and ends in early January of the next year. See the full NFC pay period calendar for 2027 for every 2027 date and the use-or-lose leave deadline.

How to work out the pay period number yourself

  1. Find the start of the leave year: Sunday, January 11, 2026; Sunday, January 10, 2027; or Sunday, January 9, 2028.
  2. Count the days from that Sunday to your date.
  3. Divide by 14, drop the remainder and add 1. That is the pay period number. The remainder plus 1 is the day of the pay period (days 1 to 7 are week 1).

Example: December 1, 2026 is 324 days after January 11, 2026. 324 ÷ 14 = 23 remainder 2, so it is pay period 24, day 3.

Frequently asked questions

What pay period is it right now?

Open this page and the calculator shows today's federal pay period, the day of the period, and the next NFC payday. Pay periods are numbered 1 to 26 within each leave year.

What pay period is December 2026?

December 2026 covers the end of pay period 23 (Nov 15 to 28), pay period 24 (Nov 29 to Dec 12), pay period 25 (Dec 13 to 26) and the start of pay period 26 (Dec 27, 2026 to Jan 9, 2027).

When does pay period 1 of 2027 start?

Sunday, January 10, 2027. Leave year 2026 ends on Saturday, January 9, 2027 with pay period 26.

How many pay periods are left in 2026?

Leave year 2026 has 26 pay periods ending January 9, 2027. The calculator shows how many remain from any date you choose.

Is it a pay week this week?

For NFC-paid employees, pay is deposited on the Monday of the second week after a pay period ends. The next paydays are listed in the calculator.

Do all federal agencies use the same pay periods?

Yes. Executive branch agencies use the same biweekly pay periods and leave year. The pay date can differ by payroll provider (NFC, DFAS, IBC or GSA).
