The short answer

How much estimated tax should you pay?

If you are self-employed or have income with no tax withheld, the IRS expects you to pay tax during the year in four installments. The last 2026 payment is due January 15, 2027. To avoid a penalty, pay at least 90% of your 2026 tax or 100% of your 2025 tax (110% if your 2025 adjusted gross income was over $150,000), whichever is smaller.

A single freelancer with $60,000 of profit and no other income owes about $12,037 in 2026 federal tax: $8,478 of self-employment tax and $3,559 of income tax. That is roughly $3,009 a quarter.

How the calculation works

  • Self-employment tax: 15.3% of 92.35% of your profit: 12.4% for Social Security on earnings up to the 2026 wage base of $184,500 (reduced by any W-2 wages), plus 2.9% for Medicare with no cap. No self-employment tax is due if net earnings are under $400.
  • Additional Medicare tax: 0.9% on wages and self-employment earnings above $200,000 ($250,000 married filing jointly).
  • Income tax: half of your self-employment tax is deducted from income, then the 2026 standard deduction ($16,100 single, $32,200 married, $24,150 head of household) and the 20% qualified business income deduction. The rest is taxed at the 2026 rates of 10% to 37%.
  • Qualified business income deduction: 20% of business profit (after half of self-employment tax), limited to 20% of taxable income. Above $201,750 of taxable income ($403,500 married) the calculator phases it out over the next $75,000 ($150,000), which is how it works for service businesses and businesses with no employees or property.

How to pay estimated tax

The quickest options are IRS Direct Pay from a bank account, your IRS online account, or EFTPS. Choose "estimated tax" and tax year 2026 for the January 15 payment. You can also mail a Form 1040-ES voucher with a check. If you have a job as well, you can instead raise your withholding with a new Form W-4; withholding counts as if it were paid evenly through the year.

Most states with an income tax also expect quarterly payments on similar dates. Check your state revenue department.

Sources: IRS Form 1040-ES, IRS Publication 505, Rev. Proc. 2025-32 and the Social Security wage base.

Frequently asked questions

When is the next estimated tax payment due?

The fourth 2026 payment is due January 15, 2027. Payments for 2027 income start April 15, 2027.

Who has to pay quarterly estimated taxes?

Anyone who expects to owe at least $1,000 in federal tax for the year after withholding and credits, typically freelancers, contractors, gig workers, landlords and people with large investment income.

How do I avoid the underpayment penalty?

Pay, through withholding and estimated payments, at least 90% of this year's tax or 100% of last year's tax (110% if last year's adjusted gross income was over $150,000, or $75,000 married filing separately), whichever is less. You also owe no penalty if your balance due after withholding is under $1,000.

How much should I set aside for taxes as a freelancer?

For federal tax, many freelancers with $40,000 to $100,000 of profit end up owing roughly 18% to 23% of profit, because self-employment tax adds about 14% on top of income tax. Use the calculator for your own numbers, and add your state tax.

Can I pay all my estimated tax in January?

You can, but the IRS can still charge a penalty for the earlier quarters you underpaid, because each installment is due on its own date. Raising W-2 withholding late in the year is different: withholding is treated as paid evenly through the year.

Does the calculator include state tax?

No. It estimates federal income tax and self-employment tax only.
