How to use the add or subtract time

Combine two durations or subtract one duration from another and normalize the result into days, hours and minutes.

Formula or method

Each duration is converted to total minutes, the selected operation is applied, and the result is normalized.

Worked examples

Add two durations

2 hours 30 minutes plus 1 hour 45 minutes equals 4 hours 15 minutes.

Subtract tracked time

8 hours minus 1 hour 20 minutes equals 6 hours 40 minutes.

A negative difference

3 hours minus 4 hours returns negative 1 hour rather than wrapping to a clock time.

Adding times by hand

  1. Add the hours and the minutes separately: 2 h 45 m + 1 h 30 m = 3 h 75 m.
  2. Convert every 60 minutes into an hour: 75 minutes = 1 h 15 m.
  3. Total: 4 hours 15 minutes.

To subtract, borrow 60 minutes from the hours when needed: 5 h 10 m − 2 h 40 m becomes 4 h 70 m − 2 h 40 m = 2 h 30 m.

Frequently asked questions

Is this for durations or times of day?

It operates on durations. Use Time Duration to compare a start clock time with an end clock time.

What happens when minutes exceed 59?

The calculator normalizes every 60 minutes into one additional hour.

Can the result be negative?

Yes. A subtraction can produce a negative duration when the second value is larger.

Can I add durations longer than one day?

Yes. The normalized result can include days, hours and minutes.

How do I add hours and minutes?

Add hours and minutes separately, then convert each 60 minutes into one hour. 2 h 45 m + 1 h 30 m = 4 h 15 m.

How do I write 1 hour 30 minutes in decimal?

1.5 hours. Divide the minutes by 60: 30 ÷ 60 = 0.5.

How do I subtract time?

Subtract minutes from minutes and hours from hours, borrowing 60 minutes from the hours when the minutes would go negative.

Limitations

This tool works with durations rather than clock times or dates.
