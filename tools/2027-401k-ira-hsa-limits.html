The short answer

2027 contribution limits at a glance

In 2027 you can put up to $25,500 into a 401(k), 403(b), 457 or the Thrift Savings Plan, up from $24,500. Workers 50 and older can add $8,500 (total $34,000), and those aged 60 to 63 can add $11,750 (total $37,250). The IRA limit stays at $7,500 ($8,600 at 50+). HSA limits rise to $4,500 for self-only coverage and $9,000 for family coverage.

To max out a 401(k) in 2027 you need to contribute $980.77 every two weeks (26 paychecks) or $2,125 a month.

To use an HSA in 2027 your health plan must have a deductible of at least $1,750 (self-only) or $3,500 (family), with out-of-pocket costs capped at $8,700 or $17,400.

Good to know

  • Employer matches don't count toward your $25,500 employee limit.
  • Roth catch-up rule: if your prior-year Social Security wages from the employer were above the IRS threshold ($150,000 for 2026), catch-up contributions must be Roth.
  • Raising your contribution lowers your taxable pay. See how it affects take-home pay with the 2027 paycheck calculator.
  • Deadlines: 401(k) deferrals must come out of 2027 paychecks; IRA and HSA contributions for 2027 can be made until the April 2028 tax deadline.

Frequently asked questions

What is the 401(k) limit for 2027?

$25,500 for employee deferrals, up from $24,500 in 2026. It applies to 401(k), 403(b), most 457 plans and the federal Thrift Savings Plan.

What is the 401(k) catch-up limit for 2027?

$8,500 for ages 50 and older, for a total of $34,000. Workers aged 60 to 63 can contribute $11,750 instead, for a total of $37,250.

What is the IRA limit for 2027?

$7,500, the same as 2026, plus a $1,100 catch-up at age 50 and older ($8,600 total).

What are the 2027 HSA limits?

$4,500 for self-only coverage and $9,000 for family coverage, plus $1,000 if you are 55 or older.

How much should I contribute per paycheck to max my 401(k) in 2027?

$980.77 per biweekly paycheck, $1,062.50 twice a month, $2,125 monthly or $490.38 weekly if you are under 50.

What is the Roth IRA income limit for 2027?

Contributions phase out between $158,000 and $173,000 of modified AGI for single filers and between $250,000 and $260,000 for married couples filing jointly.
