The short answer

How many working days are in 2027?

250 working days in the US: 261 weekdays minus 11 federal holidays. That is 2,000 working hours at 8 hours a day.

Federal government employees get one more day off. New Year's Day 2028 falls on a Saturday, so it is observed on Friday, December 31, 2027, leaving federal workers 249 working days in 2027. Most private employers observe fewer than 11 holidays, often 6 to 10, so their total is usually a little higher.

2027 US federal holidays

When a federal holiday falls on a Saturday, federal employees get the Friday before off. When it falls on a Sunday, they get the Monday after. Banks follow a slightly different rule: they close the Monday after a Sunday holiday, but stay open on the Friday before a Saturday holiday.

How to use these numbers

Real hourly rate

A $60,000 salary is $28.74 per paid hour (2,088 paid hours). If you take 10 vacation days and your employer observes 11 holidays, you actually work 1,920 hours, so your effective rate is $31.25.

Project planning

Use the monthly totals to estimate capacity. A team of five working full-time has 115 person-days in March but only 95 in January.

Contractor billing

At $50 per hour and 250 working days of 8 hours, maximum billable revenue is $100,000 before time off, admin time and gaps between projects.

Assumptions and limitations

Working days are Monday to Friday minus the 11 US federal holidays as observed by federal employees. State holidays, company-specific days off and shift schedules aren't included. Use the calculator above to adjust for your own holidays and leave.

Frequently asked questions

How many working days are in 2027?

250 working days in the US, based on 261 weekdays minus 11 federal holidays. Federal employees have 249 because New Year's Day 2028 is observed on Friday, December 31, 2027.

How many working hours are in 2027?

2,000 hours at 8 hours a day across 250 working days. Counting all 261 weekdays as paid time gives 2,088 paid hours.

Which month has the most working days in 2027?

March, with 23 working days. January and February have the fewest, with 19 each.

Which 2027 federal holidays fall on a weekend?

Juneteenth (Saturday, June 19) is observed Friday, June 18. Independence Day (Sunday, July 4) is observed Monday, July 5. Christmas (Saturday, December 25) is observed Friday, December 24.

Is December 31, 2027 a holiday?

For federal employees, yes. New Year's Day 2028 falls on a Saturday, so it is observed on Friday, December 31, 2027. Banks stay open that day.

How many weekdays are in 2027?

261. 2027 starts on a Friday and is not a leap year, so it has 52 full weeks plus one extra Friday.

How many bank business days are in 2027?

252. Federal Reserve Banks close on 9 weekday holidays in 2027 but stay open on Friday, June 18, Friday, December 24 and Friday, December 31, when Saturday holidays are observed by federal employees.

Why do some websites say 249 working days in 2027?

They follow the federal employee calendar, which also gives Friday, December 31, 2027 off for New Year's Day 2028. Most private employers count 250 or more.

How many working days are in Q1 2027?

61 working days from January to March, after Martin Luther King Jr. Day and Presidents Day.
