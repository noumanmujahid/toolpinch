The short answer

When do military members get paid in 2027?

Active duty service members are paid on the 1st and 15th of each month. When either date falls on a weekend or federal holiday, pay arrives the business day before. In 2027 that happens 7 times, starting with the January 1 payday, which is paid on Thursday, December 31, 2026.

The January 1, 2028 payday moves to Thursday, December 30, 2027, because January 1 is a Saturday and Friday, December 31 is the observed New Year's Day holiday. Many banks and credit unions release military direct deposits a day or two early.

2027 military retiree and annuitant pay dates

DFAS pays retired pay and Survivor Benefit Plan annuities monthly for the previous month. Pay is due on the 1st. If the 1st is a weekend or holiday, retirees are paid the last business day of the month before and annuitants the first business day after.

Good to know

  • Reserve and Guard drill pay is paid after drills are processed, not on a fixed 1st/15th schedule; check your Leave and Earnings Statement in myPay.
  • 2027 military pay raise: the military raise is set separately from civilian pay in the National Defense Authorization Act and takes effect January 1, 2027. Civilian GS pay is covered on the GS pay scale 2027 page.
  • Government shutdowns can delay military pay unless Congress provides funding; current federal funding runs through December 11, 2026.

Frequently asked questions

When is military payday in January 2027?

The January 1 payday is paid on Thursday, December 31, 2026 because New Year's Day is a holiday. The mid-month payday is Friday, January 15, 2027.

What happens if the 1st or 15th is on a weekend?

Active duty pay is deposited on the last business day before the weekend or holiday.

When do military retirees get paid in 2027?

On the 1st of each month for the previous month, or the last business day before if the 1st is a weekend or holiday. Annuitants are paid the first business day after.

Is the January 1, 2028 military payday early?

Yes. It is paid on Thursday, December 30, 2027, because January 1, 2028 is a Saturday and Friday, December 31, 2027 is a federal holiday.

Do banks pay military members early?

Many banks and credit unions release military direct deposits one or two business days early, but the official pay date is the one listed here.
