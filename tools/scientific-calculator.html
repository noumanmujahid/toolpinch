The short answer

How it works

Type an expression and the calculator works it out using the standard order of operations: brackets first, then powers and roots, then multiplication and division, then addition and subtraction. So 2 + 3 × 4 = 14, while (2 + 3) × 4 = 20.

Angles are in radians. To use degrees, multiply by pi/180: sin(30 × pi/180) = 0.5. One full turn is 2π radians = 360°.

Worked examples

Area of a circle

Radius 7 cm: pi * 7^2 = 153.94 cm².

Compound growth

$1,000 at 5% for 10 years: 1000 * 1.05^10 = $1,628.89.

Pythagoras

Sides 6 and 8: sqrt(6^2 + 8^2) = 10.

Height of a ramp

4 m long at 10°: 4 * sin(10 * pi/180) = 0.69 m.

Order-of-operations traps

  • Division and multiplication go left to right. 8 / 2 * 4 = 16, not 1. Add brackets if you mean 8 / (2 × 4).
  • Negative numbers and powers. Always put a negative base in brackets: (−3)^2 = 9. Without brackets the expression is ambiguous and may be rejected.
  • Fractions in exponents. 8^1/3 is read as (8^1) / 3. Write 8^(1/3) for the cube root.
  • Rounding. Results are shown to a sensible number of decimal places; very large or tiny numbers may appear in scientific notation.

Frequently asked questions

Does the scientific calculator use degrees or radians?

Radians. To work in degrees, multiply the angle by pi/180, for example sin(30 * pi/180) = 0.5.

How do I type a square root or cube root?

Use sqrt(x) for a square root and x^(1/3) for a cube root. Any nth root is x^(1/n).

What order does the calculator use?

Brackets, then powers and roots, then multiplication and division from left to right, then addition and subtraction from left to right.

Why does 8 / 2 * 4 equal 16?

Multiplication and division have equal priority and are worked left to right: 8 / 2 = 4, then 4 × 4 = 16.

How do I use pi?

Type pi or tap the π key. pi * 5^2 gives the area of a circle with radius 5.

Is my calculation sent anywhere?

No. Expressions are evaluated in your browser.
