The short answer

How it works

Mean = sum ÷ count. Median = the middle value when sorted (the average of the two middle values if the count is even). Mode = the most frequent value. Range = largest − smallest. For 2, 4, 4, 4, 5, 5, 7, 9: mean 5, median 4.5, mode 4, range 7.

Worked example with an odd count

House prices (in $1,000s): 210, 245, 260, 275, 940.

  • Mean = 1,930 ÷ 5 = 386
  • Median = the third value = 260
  • Mode = none (every value appears once)
  • Range = 940 − 210 = 730

One expensive house lifts the mean far above what most of the homes cost. This is why house prices and incomes are usually reported as medians.

Which measure should you use?

MeasureBest forWeakness
MeanSymmetric data without extreme values; totals and budgetsPulled by outliers
MedianSkewed data such as incomes, prices, waiting timesIgnores how far the extremes are
ModeCategories and the most common choice (shoe size, survey answer)There may be none, or several
RangeA quick sense of spreadDepends only on the two extreme values

Finding the median step by step

  1. Sort the values from smallest to largest.
  2. If the count is odd, the median is the middle value: position (n + 1) ÷ 2.
  3. If the count is even, average the two middle values: for 3, 7, 8, 12 the median is (7 + 8) ÷ 2 = 7.5.

A dataset can have more than one mode (bimodal or multimodal) or no mode at all when every value appears the same number of times. For a fuller picture of spread, add the standard deviation.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find the mean, median and mode?

Mean: add all values and divide by the count. Median: sort the values and take the middle one (or the average of the two middle ones). Mode: the value that appears most often.

What is the median of an even set of numbers?

The average of the two middle values after sorting. For 3, 7, 8 and 12 the median is 7.5.

Can there be more than one mode?

Yes. If two or more values tie for most frequent, the data is bimodal or multimodal. If every value appears equally often, there is no mode.

When is the median better than the mean?

When the data is skewed or has outliers, such as incomes or house prices. The median shows the typical value without being pulled by a few extreme numbers.

How do I calculate the range?

Subtract the smallest value from the largest. For 2 to 9 the range is 7.

What is the difference between mean and average?

In everyday use they mean the same thing: the arithmetic mean. Median and mode are other kinds of average.
