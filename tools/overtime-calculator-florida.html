The short answer

How does overtime work in Florida?

Florida has no separate state overtime law for most private employers, so the federal Fair Labor Standards Act applies: 1.5× the regular rate for hours over 40 in a workweek. There is no daily overtime.

Florida overtime rules at a glance

Weekly overtime1.5× after 40 hours in a workweek
Daily overtimeNone
Weekends and holidaysNo extra pay required by law
Exempt salary minimum (federal)$684 a week ($35,568 a year)

Tipped employees in Florida also get overtime. It is calculated on the full minimum wage, not the reduced tipped cash wage.

Worked example

At $18 an hour, a 45-hour week pays 40 × $18 = $720 regular plus 5 × $27 = $135 overtime, a total of $855 before taxes.

Does the "no tax on overtime" deduction apply?

Starting with 2025 tax returns, workers can deduct qualified overtime pay on their federal income tax return, up to $12,500 a year ($25,000 for married couples filing jointly). The deduction phases out above $150,000 of modified adjusted gross income ($300,000 joint). Only the extra part of overtime pay, the "half" in time-and-a-half, counts, and only for overtime the federal Fair Labor Standards Act requires: hours over 40 in a workweek. Social Security and Medicare are still withheld from overtime pay. The calculator above estimates the federal premium for your week.

Frequently asked questions

Does Florida have its own overtime law?

Not for most private employers. Florida follows the federal FLSA, which requires 1.5x pay for hours over 40 in a workweek.

Is there daily overtime in Florida?

No. Only hours over 40 in a workweek trigger overtime under federal law.

Do I get overtime for working holidays in Florida?

Not by law. Holiday premium pay depends on your employer's policy or contract.

Can hours be averaged over two weeks in Florida?

No. Each 7-day workweek stands alone for overtime, even if you are paid every two weeks.
