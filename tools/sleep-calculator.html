Sleep Calculator

Choose your target time and preferred sleep length.

Understand the result

How to use this tool

Estimate one bedtime or wake-up time from a selected number of 90-minute sleep cycles.

Formula or method

Selected cycles are multiplied by 90 minutes. Bedtime calculations also subtract an estimated 15 minutes for falling asleep.

Worked examples

Example 1

Five cycles represent about 7 hours 30 minutes of sleep.

Example 2

Waking at 7:00 AM after five cycles suggests a bedtime around 11:15 PM with the settling estimate.

Frequently asked questions

What does the sleep calculator calculate?

Estimate one bedtime or wake-up time from a selected number of 90-minute sleep cycles.

How is the result calculated?

Selected cycles are multiplied by 90 minutes. Bedtime calculations also subtract an estimated 15 minutes for falling asleep.

Are my inputs uploaded?

No. This calculation runs in the current browser and does not require an account.

What should I check before using the result?

Sleep cycles and individual sleep needs vary; this is not medical guidance.
