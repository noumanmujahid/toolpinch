How to use the sleep calculator

Estimate one bedtime or wake-up time from a selected number of 90-minute sleep cycles.

Formula or method

Selected cycles are multiplied by 90 minutes. Bedtime calculations also subtract an estimated 15 minutes for falling asleep.

Worked examples

Wake at 7:00 AM

Five 90-minute cycles plus a 15-minute settling estimate suggests going to bed around 11:15 PM.

Plan six cycles

Six cycles represent about nine hours of sleep, before adding time to fall asleep.

Calculate a wake time

Choose a bedtime and four cycles to estimate a wake time about six hours later.

Frequently asked questions

Why does the calculator use 90-minute cycles?

Ninety minutes is a common planning estimate, but real sleep cycles vary between people and across the night.

Is 15 minutes always enough to fall asleep?

No. It is only a default settling estimate and may be too short or long for you.

Does the result guarantee I will wake refreshed?

No. Sleep quality depends on health, schedule, environment and many other factors.

Is this medical advice?

No. Persistent sleep problems should be discussed with a qualified healthcare professional.

Limitations

Sleep cycles and individual sleep needs vary; this is not medical guidance.
