What Is the Slope Between These Two Points?

Find slope, rise, run and the line equation from two points.

Slope Calculator

Enter values to see one clear answer.

Method

Slope m = (y₂ − y₁) ÷ (x₂ − x₁). Substitute either point into y = mx + b to find the intercept.

Example

Points (1,2) and (5,10) have slope (10 − 2) ÷ (5 − 1) = 2.
