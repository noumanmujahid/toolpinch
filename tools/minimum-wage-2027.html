The short answer

Which states raise the minimum wage in 2027?

16 states raise their minimum wage on January 1, 2027: Arizona, California, Colorado, Connecticut, Maine, Michigan, Minnesota, Montana, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Rhode Island, South Dakota, Vermont, Virginia, Washington. The highest state rate is Washington at $17.73 an hour. The biggest jump is Michigan, up $1.27 to $15.00. The federal minimum wage stays at $7.25, which is still the rate in 20 states.

Alaska rises to $15.00 on July 1, 2027, and Oregon and Washington, D.C. adjust their rates for inflation every July 1. New York's planned 2027 increase was suspended, so rates stay at $17.00 in New York City, Long Island and Westchester and $16.00 in the rest of the state.

What employers should do before January 1

Update pay rates

The new rate applies to hours worked from January 1. If your pay period crosses New Year's Day, pay the old rate for December hours and the new rate for January hours.

Check tipped and salaried staff

Tipped cash wages and the minimum salary for exempt employees are tied to the minimum wage in several states, including California, Colorado, New Jersey and Washington.

Replace your posters

States that change their rate publish a new minimum wage poster. Display the 2027 version where employees can see it.

Sources and limitations

Rates come from the U.S. Department of Labor and announcements from state labor departments and governors' offices, checked in November 2026. Vermont's 2027 rate is a projection until the state publishes it. This page is general information, not legal advice; check your state labor department for coverage rules, local rates and special rates.

Frequently asked questions

Which states raise the minimum wage on January 1, 2027?

16 states: Arizona, California, Colorado, Connecticut, Maine, Michigan, Minnesota, Montana, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Rhode Island, South Dakota, Vermont, Virginia, Washington.

What is the highest state minimum wage in 2027?

Washington, at $17.73 an hour from January 1, 2027. Washington, D.C. is higher at $18.40, and some cities, such as Seattle and Denver, are higher still.

Is the federal minimum wage going up in 2027?

No. The federal minimum wage has been $7.25 an hour since July 2009, and no increase has been enacted. It remains the minimum in 20 states.

What is the California minimum wage in 2027?

$17.40 an hour for all employers from January 1, 2027, up from $16.90. Many California cities set higher local rates.

What is the New York minimum wage in 2027?

It stays at $17.00 an hour in New York City, Long Island and Westchester and $16.00 in the rest of the state. The inflation increase planned for 2027 was suspended.

What is the Michigan minimum wage in 2027?

$15.00 an hour from January 1, 2027, up from $13.73. It is the largest increase of any state in 2027.

How much is $15 an hour a year?

$31,200 a year before tax at 40 hours a week for 52 weeks, or $600 a week.

Does the minimum wage go up in Florida in 2027?

No. Florida reached $15.00 on September 30, 2026. The next increase, based on inflation, takes effect January 1, 2028.
