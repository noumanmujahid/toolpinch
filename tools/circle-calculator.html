What Are This Circle’s Missing Measurements?

Circle Calculator

Enter values to see one clear answer.

Method

Diameter = 2r, circumference = 2πr, and area = πr². Any known measurement is first converted to radius.

Example

A circle with radius 5 m has circumference about 31.416 m and area about 78.54 m².
