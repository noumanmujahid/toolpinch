The short answer

How do you compare unit prices?

Divide the price by the quantity. A $4.99 12-ounce bag costs 4.99 ÷ 12 = 41.6¢ per ounce; a $8.49 24-ounce bag costs 35.4¢ per ounce, so the bigger bag is 15% cheaper per ounce. When sizes use different units, convert first: 1 lb = 16 oz = 453.6 g, 1 kg = 2.2 lb, and 1 US gallon = 128 fl oz = 3.785 litres.

When bigger is not cheaper

  • Sales on small sizes. A discounted small pack can beat the regular price of the family size.
  • Multi-buy offers. "2 for $5" on 10-oz cans is 25¢ per ounce; compare it with the large can's unit price rather than assuming the deal wins.
  • Waste. The cheapest unit price only saves money if you use it all before it spoils or expires.
  • Shelf labels. Shelf tags show unit prices, but stores sometimes use different units for similar products (per ounce versus per count); convert before comparing.

Worked examples

Coffee

$11.99 for 2 lb (32 oz) is 37.5¢ per ounce, about 6% more than the 24-oz bag at 35.4¢.

Laundry detergent

$13.97 for 92 fl oz is 15.2¢/fl oz; $19.97 for 150 fl oz is 13.3¢/fl oz. The large bottle saves about 12%.

Paper towels

Compare per sheet or per square foot, not per roll; "double rolls" vary between brands.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate unit price?

Divide the price by the quantity in the same unit. $8.49 for 24 oz is 8.49 ÷ 24 = $0.354 per ounce.

How do I compare prices in ounces and grams?

Convert to one unit first: 1 ounce = 28.35 grams. The calculator converts automatically.

Is the bigger size always cheaper?

No. Sales on smaller sizes and multi-buy offers can make smaller packs cheaper per unit, so compare the unit price each time.

How many ounces are in a pound?

16 ounces (weight).

Can I compare a weight with a volume?

Not directly. Ounces of weight and fluid ounces of volume measure different things; compare products sold in the same kind of unit.
