The short answer

How much tax is taken from a bonus?

United States: employers usually withhold a flat 22% federal tax on bonuses (37% on any amount over $1 million), plus 7.65% Social Security and Medicare and any state tax. A $5,000 bonus becomes about $3,518 before state tax.
United Kingdom: a bonus is taxed as ordinary pay at your marginal rates. A basic-rate taxpayer keeps 72% (20% tax + 8% National Insurance); a higher-rate taxpayer keeps 58% (40% + 2%).
Pakistan: a bonus is added to your annual salary and taxed at your slab rate, from 0% to 35% under the tax year 2027 slabs.

Bonuses are not taxed more than salary; they only look that way because withholding is applied to the bonus in one go.

United States: bonus withholding in 2026

The IRS treats bonuses as supplemental wages. Most employers use the percentage method: a flat 22% federal withholding on the bonus, separate from your regular paycheck. Some combine the bonus with your regular pay and withhold as if you earned that much every period (the aggregate method), which can withhold even more.

State tax: many states also have a flat supplemental rate, for example California withholds 10.23% on bonuses. Enter your state's rate in the calculator. Nine states have no wage income tax.

Under section 149 of the Income Tax Ordinance, employers deduct tax on the bonus based on your estimated annual salary including the bonus. A bonus that pushes your annual salary into a higher slab is taxed at the higher rate only on the part that falls into that slab.

Ways to keep more of a bonus

  • Pension or retirement contributions: a 401(k) contribution from the bonus (US) or a salary sacrifice into a pension (UK) is taken before income tax.
  • Timing: if your income will be lower next year, a bonus paid in January rather than December falls into the lower-income year.
  • Check withholding: in the US, if 22% is more than your bracket, you will get it back at tax time; adjust your W-4 if you prefer a smaller refund.

Sources: IRS Publication 15 (supplemental wages), GOV.UK income tax rates, FBR and the Finance Act 2026 slabs. Estimates only.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my bonus taxed so much?

In the US, employers usually withhold a flat 22% federal tax plus 7.65% Social Security and Medicare and state tax, so 30% to 40% can be withheld. That is withholding, not your final tax; if your actual bracket is lower, you get the difference back as a refund.

Are bonuses taxed at a higher rate than salary?

No. Bonuses are taxed as ordinary income. They can look heavily taxed because withholding is calculated on the bonus alone, or as if you earned that much every pay period.

What is the federal tax rate on bonuses in 2026?

22% for supplemental wages up to $1 million in a year and 37% on supplemental wages above $1 million, when the employer uses the percentage method.

How much of a £5,000 bonus will I keep in the UK?

About £3,600 as a basic-rate taxpayer (20% tax + 8% NI) and about £2,900 as a higher-rate taxpayer (40% tax + 2% NI), in England, Wales or Northern Ireland.

How is a bonus taxed in Pakistan?

It is added to your salary income for the tax year and taxed at your slab rate. Under the tax year 2027 slabs, rates run from 0% to 35%.

Can I avoid tax on my bonus?

You cannot avoid it entirely, but contributing part of it to a 401(k) in the US or a pension through salary sacrifice in the UK reduces the tax.
