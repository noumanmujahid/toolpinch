What Score Do You Need on the Final?

Find the final-exam score needed to reach a target course grade.

Final Grade Calculator

Enter values to see one clear answer.

The short answer

How it works

Required final score = (target grade − current grade × (1 − final weight)) ÷ final weight. With 82% going into a final worth 30%, you need (85 − 82 × 0.70) ÷ 0.30 = 92% on the final to finish with an 85%.

What you need on the final

For a student with 82% going into a final worth 30% of the course grade:

Target course gradeScore needed on final
90% (A−)108.7% (not possible)
85%92.0%
80% (B−)75.3%
70% (C−)42.0%
60% (D−)8.7%

A result above 100% means the target cannot be reached on the final alone; a result below 0% means you have already secured it.

Worked examples

Heavy final

Current 75%, final worth 40%, target 80%: (80 − 75 × 0.6) ÷ 0.4 = 87.5%.

Light final

Current 91%, final worth 15%, target 90%: (90 − 91 × 0.85) ÷ 0.15 = 84.3%.

Points-based course

If grades are in points, use your percentage so far: 412 of 480 points = 85.8%, then apply the formula.

Things to check first

  • Is your current grade already weighted? Learning platforms usually show a weighted current grade; use that number.
  • Exam floors and drop rules. Some courses require a minimum score on the final to pass, or drop your lowest quiz, which changes the maths.
  • Letter-grade cut-offs. Check your syllabus: an 89.5% is rounded to an A− in some courses and not in others.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate what I need on my final exam?

Subtract your current grade times the share of the course already completed from your target grade, then divide by the final's weight. With 82% now, a 30% final and an 85% target: (85 − 82 × 0.7) ÷ 0.3 = 92%.

What if the calculator says I need more than 100%?

Your target is out of reach through the final alone, unless extra credit is available. Try a lower target to see what you can still achieve.

How much will the final exam change my grade?

Each point on the final moves your course grade by the final's weight. On a 30% final, scoring 10 points higher raises your course grade by 3 points.

What if my class uses points instead of percentages?

Convert to a percentage first by dividing the points earned by the points possible so far, and use the final's share of total points as its weight.

Can I fail a class by failing the final?

Yes, if the final carries enough weight or the course requires a minimum exam score. Use the calculator with a 60% or 70% target to see the score you need to pass.

Is my current grade the same as my grade so far?

Use the weighted grade for the work completed. If your platform shows a grade that already treats missing work as zero, it will understate your standing.
