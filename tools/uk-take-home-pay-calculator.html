The short answer

How much tax will I pay in the UK in 2026/27?

The first £12,570 is tax-free. In England, Wales and Northern Ireland you then pay 20% up to £50,270, 40% up to £125,140 and 45% above. National Insurance is 8% on earnings between £12,570 and £50,270 and 2% above. On a £35,000 salary you pay £4,486 income tax and £1,794 National Insurance, and take home £28,720 a year (£2,393 a month) before pension.

The rates and thresholds are unchanged from 2025/26: the government has frozen them until April 2031. The Autumn Budget on 28 October 2026 made no change to income tax or National Insurance on wages for 2026/27 or 2027/28.

The £100,000 trap: the Personal Allowance falls by £1 for every £2 of income over £100,000 and disappears at £125,140. Income in that range is taxed at an effective 60% (62% in Scotland, before NI). Pension contributions reduce the income used for this test, which is why many people in this band pay extra into a pension.

National Insurance and student loans

Employees pay Class 1 National Insurance at 8% on earnings between the Primary Threshold (£12,570, or £1,048 a month) and the Upper Earnings Limit (£50,270), and 2% above. NI is the same in every part of the UK and is worked out on each pay period separately.

How the calculator works

Pension contributions are treated as a net pay arrangement: they come off your salary before income tax but not before National Insurance. With salary sacrifice, NI would also be lower. The calculator works on annual figures with a standard 1257L tax code and no other income, benefits in kind or Marriage Allowance. Your payslip can differ slightly because tax and NI are worked out each pay period.

Frequently asked questions

How much is £30,000 after tax in the UK?

In England, Wales or Northern Ireland, £25,120 a year or £2,093 a month in 2026/27, after £3,486 income tax and £1,394 National Insurance, with no pension or student loan.

How much is £50,000 after tax?

£39,520 a year or £3,293 a month, after £7,486 income tax and £2,994 National Insurance.

How much is £100,000 after tax?

£68,557 a year or £5,713 a month in England, Wales or Northern Ireland, before pension contributions.

What is the Personal Allowance for 2026/27?

£12,570, the same as since April 2021. It is frozen until April 2031. It is reduced by £1 for every £2 of income over £100,000.

What are the Scottish income tax rates for 2026/27?

19% starter rate from £12,571 to £16,537, 20% basic to £29,526, 21% intermediate to £43,662, 42% higher to £75,000, 45% advanced to £125,140 and 48% top rate above.

What is the National Insurance rate in 2026/27?

Employees pay 8% on earnings between £12,570 and £50,270 a year and 2% on earnings above £50,270.

Does a pension contribution reduce tax?

Yes. Contributions taken from pay before tax reduce your income tax. Under salary sacrifice they also reduce National Insurance.

When does the 2026/27 tax year run?

From 6 April 2026 to 5 April 2027.
