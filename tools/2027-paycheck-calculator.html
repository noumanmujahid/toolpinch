The short answer

Will my paycheck go up in 2027?

For most people, yes, slightly, even without a raise. The 2027 tax brackets are about 3% wider and the standard deduction rises to $16,600 for single filers ($33,200 married), so less federal income tax is withheld. At a $75,000 salary, single, federal income tax falls from $7,670 to $7,390 a year: about +$10.79 every two weeks.

High earners see a partial offset: the Social Security wage base rises from $184,500 to $192,300, so anyone earning above $184,500 pays up to $483.60 more Social Security tax in 2027, usually noticed late in the year.

Other things that can change your first 2027 paycheck

  • Health insurance premiums often rise in January after open enrollment, which can cancel out the tax savings.
  • State income tax brackets and rates change in many states; see the US paycheck calculator for your state.
  • Your 401(k) election may reset or auto-increase. If you want to max out at $25,500, divide by your number of paychecks.
  • Federal employees: the 2027 GS raise is proposed at 0%; see the GS pay scale 2027. Social Security recipients get a 3.6% COLA.
  • A new W-4 is only needed if your situation changed. Your existing W-4 automatically uses the 2027 tables.

Frequently asked questions

Will my paycheck be bigger in 2027?

For most salaried workers with the same pay, slightly, because the 2027 brackets and standard deduction are higher. At $75,000 single, take-home rises about $280 a year.

What is the 2027 standard deduction?

$16,600 for single filers and married filing separately, $33,200 for married filing jointly, and $24,925 for head of household.

What is the Social Security wage base for 2027?

$192,300, up from $184,500 in 2026. Wages above that are not subject to the 6.2% Social Security tax; Medicare tax has no limit.

What is the 401(k) limit for 2027?

$25,500, up from $24,500. Workers 50 and older can add $8,500 (total $34,000), and those aged 60 to 63 can add $11,750 instead.

Do I need a new W-4 for 2027?

No, unless your situation changed. Employers apply the new 2027 withholding tables to your existing W-4 automatically.

Why is my January paycheck smaller?

Usually because health insurance premiums or other benefit costs went up, a 401(k) contribution increased, or your state changed its withholding. Federal income tax withholding goes down slightly for most people in 2027.
