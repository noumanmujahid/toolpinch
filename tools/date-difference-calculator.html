How to use the date difference calculator

Compare two calendar dates in total days, full weeks and weekdays for deadlines, travel or project planning.

Formula or method

Dates are normalized to calendar values; the difference in milliseconds is divided by one day. Weekdays count Monday through Friday.

Worked examples

One full week

September 1 to September 8 is seven elapsed calendar days, or one full week.

A project deadline

October 2 to October 20 is 18 calendar days; the weekday count excludes Saturdays and Sundays.

Dates in reverse order

If the dates are entered in reverse, the tool still reports the absolute distance between them.

A year has 365 days, or 366 in a leap year. Leap years are divisible by 4, except century years that are not divisible by 400: 2028 is a leap year, 2100 will not be. A year has 52 weeks and 1 day (2 in a leap year), which is why a date moves one weekday later each year.

Working days in a year

2026 and 2027 each have 261 weekdays (Monday to Friday); 2028 has 260. Subtract public holidays to get working days: the US has 11 federal holidays, so a federal employee has about 250 working days a year; England and Wales have 8 bank holidays.

Counting tips

  • Elapsed versus inclusive. March 1 to March 10 is 9 days elapsed, but 10 days if you count both the first and last day (for example, a hotel stay is counted in nights, a festival in days).
  • Notice periods and deadlines. Check whether the rule counts calendar days or business days and whether the day of notice itself counts.
  • Age in days. Someone born 10,000 days ago is about 27 years and 4 months old.

Frequently asked questions

Does the result include both the start and end date?

The main result measures elapsed time between the two dates. Counting both dates as occupied calendar dates would add one.

What is the difference between calendar days and weekdays?

Calendar days include every day. Weekdays include Monday through Friday only.

Are public holidays removed?

No. Holiday calendars vary by country and region, so only weekends are excluded from the weekday figure.

Can daylight saving change the result?

Dates are normalized for calendar comparison, which avoids most one-hour daylight-saving shifts affecting the day count.

How many weekdays are there in 2027?

261 weekdays (Monday to Friday). Subtract public holidays to get working days.

How do I count days including the start and end date?

Add 1 to the elapsed number of days. From March 1 to March 10 is 9 days elapsed, or 10 days counting both dates.

Is 2028 a leap year?

Yes. 2028 is divisible by 4 and is not a century year, so February 2028 has 29 days.

Limitations

Country-specific public holidays are not excluded.
