What Do These Numbers Have in Common?

Find the greatest common factor and least common multiple.

LCM and GCF Calculator

Enter values to see one clear answer.

Method

The GCF is the largest whole number dividing both inputs. For non-zero integers, LCM = |a × b| ÷ GCF.

Example

For 24 and 36, the GCF is 12 and the LCM is 72.
