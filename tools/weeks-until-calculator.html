How to use the weeks until calculator

Answer “How many weeks until?” for a holiday, appointment, deadline, trip or any future date.

Formula or method

Total days remaining are divided by seven; the quotient gives full weeks and the remainder gives extra days.

Worked examples

Exactly two weeks away

A target 14 days from today returns two full weeks and zero extra days.

Two weeks and three days

A target 17 days away returns two full weeks, three extra days and 17 total days.

A date tomorrow

A target tomorrow returns zero full weeks and one extra day.

Frequently asked questions

Are partial weeks rounded up?

No. The result shows complete seven-day weeks plus the remaining days, so 10 days is one week and three days.

Does the count include today?

No. It measures time from today to the target date, with today treated as day zero.

Can I choose a date in the past?

No. Use Days Since or Date Difference for dates that have already passed.

Are weekends and public holidays removed?

No. The result uses calendar days. Date Difference can also show Monday-to-Friday weekdays.

Limitations

The calculation uses your local calendar date and does not remove holidays or weekends.
