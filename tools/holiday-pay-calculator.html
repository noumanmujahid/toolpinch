The short answer

Do you get paid extra for working a holiday?

Only if your employer, contract or state requires it. Federal law does not require holiday pay or a premium for working Thanksgiving, Christmas or any other holiday. Many employers still pay time and a half or double time. At $20 an hour, 8 hours on Thanksgiving at time and a half pays $240 instead of $160.

Thanksgiving 2026 is Thursday, November 26. Christmas Day and New Year's Day both fall on a Friday this year.

How holiday pay and overtime work together

  • Overtime is based on hours actually worked. Under the Fair Labor Standards Act, non-exempt employees earn overtime for hours worked over 40 in a week. Hours you work on a holiday count; a paid holiday you take off does not.
  • Holiday premiums can offset overtime. If your employer pays at least time and a half for holiday work, federal rules let that extra premium be credited toward overtime owed for the same week, so you are not paid "time and a half on time and a half." Some employers and union contracts pay both; the calculator lets you choose.
  • Paid holidays off are usually paid at your regular rate and do not count toward the 40 hours.
  • Salaried exempt employees generally receive their normal salary for a holiday week, whether or not they work the holiday.

State holiday pay laws

Rhode Island is the only state with a general holiday premium: covered employers must pay at least time and a half for work on Sundays and state holidays, including Thanksgiving and Christmas, with exemptions for some industries. Massachusetts used to require a premium for retail work on Sundays and certain holidays, but it was phased out and ended in 2023. No other state requires holiday premium pay for private employers, although state overtime rules such as California's daily overtime still apply on holidays.

Federal government employees who work on a federal holiday receive holiday premium pay equal to their basic rate (double time) for non-overtime hours.

Frequently asked questions

Is holiday pay required by law?

Not under federal law. The Fair Labor Standards Act does not require pay for holidays or a premium for working them. Rhode Island requires time and a half for many employers; otherwise it depends on your employer's policy or contract.

How much is time and a half on a holiday?

Multiply your hourly rate by 1.5. At $20 an hour, holiday time and a half is $30 an hour, so an 8-hour shift pays $240.

Does holiday pay count toward overtime?

Hours you actually work on a holiday count toward the 40-hour overtime threshold. Paid holiday hours you take off do not. A holiday premium of at least time and a half can be credited toward overtime owed in the same week.

When is Thanksgiving 2026?

Thursday, November 26, 2026. Christmas Day is Friday, December 25, 2026, and New Year's Day is Friday, January 1, 2027.

Do part-time workers get holiday pay?

Only if the employer's policy includes them. There is no federal requirement, and many employers limit paid holidays to full-time staff.

Do salaried employees get extra pay for working a holiday?

Exempt salaried employees usually receive their normal salary regardless; extra pay or a substitute day off depends on company policy.
