What Return Did Your Investment Produce?

Calculate return on investment percentage, net gain and investment multiple.

ROI Calculator

Enter values to see one clear answer.

Method

Net gain = final value − initial investment − additional costs. ROI percentage = net gain ÷ total invested cost × 100.

Example

An investment of $10,000 that becomes $12,500 after $500 in costs has a $2,000 gain and 19.05% ROI on $10,500 invested.

Common questions

How do I calculate return on investment percentage?

Subtract the total invested cost from the final value, divide the net gain by total invested cost, then multiply by 100.

Is this an annual ROI calculator?

The result is the total ROI for the values entered. Annual ROI also needs the exact holding period and a chosen annualization method.
