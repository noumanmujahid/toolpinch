How to use this calculator

Payroll and timesheet systems often store time as decimals such as 7.75 hours. Enter the decimal to see it as hours, minutes and seconds.

Formula

Hours = whole part of the number. Minutes = decimal part × 60. Seconds = remaining fraction of a minute × 60.

Worked examples

7.75 hours

0.75 × 60 = 45, so 7.75 hours is 7 hours 45 minutes.

2.5 hours

0.5 × 60 = 30, so 2.5 hours is 2 hours 30 minutes.

8.1 hours

0.1 × 60 = 6, so 8.1 hours is 8 hours 6 minutes, not 8 hours 10 minutes.

Frequently asked questions

Why is 8.5 hours not 8 hours 50 minutes?

Decimals count in tenths of an hour, while minutes count in sixtieths. Half an hour is 0.5, which is 30 minutes.

How do I convert minutes to a decimal?

Divide the minutes by 60. For example 15 minutes is 0.25 hours.

Does my employer round decimal hours?

Some payroll systems round to the nearest tenth or quarter hour. Check your employer policy.

Assumptions and limitations

The converter shows exact values with no payroll rounding. Your employer or payroll system may round differently.
