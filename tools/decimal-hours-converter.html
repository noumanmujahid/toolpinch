How to use the decimal hours converter

Decimal hours are commonly used in payroll and invoicing. Convert the decimal portion into minutes and seconds without mistaking 0.30 hours for 30 minutes.

Formula or method

Total seconds = decimal hours × 3,600. Whole hours are removed first; the remainder is converted to minutes and seconds.

Worked examples

Two and a half hours

2.5 decimal hours equals 2 hours 30 minutes because 0.5 × 60 = 30.

A payroll entry

7.75 hours equals 7 hours 45 minutes, not 7 hours 75 minutes.

Minutes to a decimal check

15 minutes is 0.25 hour, so 8 hours 15 minutes is 8.25 decimal hours.

Common mistakes with decimal hours

Reading 8.30 as 8:30

8.30 decimal hours is 8 hours 18 minutes. 8 hours 30 minutes is 8.50. At $20 an hour, mixing them up costs $4 per shift.

Adding times as decimals

7:45 + 8:30 is not 15.75. Convert first: 7.75 + 8.50 = 16.25 hours, or 16 hours 15 minutes.

Rounding too early

Convert the weekly total once instead of rounding every shift. Rounding each shift to 0.1 hour can drift by several minutes over a week.

Frequently asked questions

Is 7.30 hours the same as 7 hours 30 minutes?

No. 0.30 of an hour is 18 minutes. Seven hours 30 minutes is 7.5 decimal hours.

How do I convert minutes into decimal hours?

Divide the minutes by 60, then add the whole hours. For example, 45 ÷ 60 = 0.75.

Why do payroll systems use decimal hours?

Decimals make multiplication by an hourly rate straightforward and avoid mixing base-60 minutes with base-10 currency.

Does the converter round seconds?

The displayed result rounds to whole seconds, so very long fractional inputs can have a small display difference.

What is 45 minutes in decimal hours?

0.75 hours, because 45 ÷ 60 = 0.75.

What is 20 minutes in decimal hours?

0.33 hours, rounded from 0.333.

How do I convert hours and minutes to decimal?

Divide the minutes by 60 and add the result to the whole hours. 6 hours 40 minutes = 6 + 40 ÷ 60 = 6.67 hours.

Limitations
