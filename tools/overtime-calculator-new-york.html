The short answer

How does overtime work in New York?

In New York, most non-exempt employees earn 1.5× their regular rate for hours over 40 in a workweek. Residential employees who live in their employer's home get overtime after 44 hours. There is no daily overtime, and hours can't be averaged across weeks.

New York overtime rules at a glance

Most employees1.5× after 40 hours in a workweek
Residential (live-in) employees1.5× after 44 hours in a workweek
Daily overtimeNone
Averaging weeksNot allowed
Waiving overtimeNot allowed

The workweek is a fixed, recurring 168-hour period. Your regular rate can't be below the New York minimum wage. If you are paid a salary for a set schedule, your regular rate is the salary divided by your scheduled hours.

Special New York rules

Exempt from federal, not from NY

Some workers who are exempt from federal overtime are still covered by New York law. They get overtime at 1.5 times the New York minimum wage instead of 1.5 times their own rate.

Weekends and nights

New York doesn't require extra pay for weekend, holiday or night work. A contract or union agreement can.

Exempt salaries

New York sets its own salary minimums for exempt executive and administrative employees, which are higher than the federal level and vary by region. Check the NY Department of Labor for current figures.

Does the "no tax on overtime" deduction apply?

Starting with 2025 tax returns, workers can deduct qualified overtime pay on their federal income tax return, up to $12,500 a year ($25,000 for married couples filing jointly). The deduction phases out above $150,000 of modified adjusted gross income ($300,000 joint). Only the extra part of overtime pay, the "half" in time-and-a-half, counts, and only for overtime the federal Fair Labor Standards Act requires: hours over 40 in a workweek. Social Security and Medicare are still withheld from overtime pay. The calculator above estimates the federal premium for your week.

Frequently asked questions

When does overtime start in New York?

After 40 hours in a workweek for most employees, or after 44 hours for residential employees who live in their employer's home.

Does New York have daily overtime?

No. Overtime is based on hours in the workweek, so a long single day doesn't trigger it on its own.

Can I agree to give up overtime in New York?

No. Employees can't waive their right to overtime pay.

Do salaried employees get overtime in New York?

Covered salaried employees do. Overtime is owed for hours over 40 even if their regular schedule is longer.
