The short answer

How do you calculate VAT?

To add VAT, multiply the net price by the rate: at 20%, £100 × 0.20 = £20 VAT, so the total is £120. To remove VAT from a price that already includes it, divide by 1 plus the rate: £120 ÷ 1.20 = £100, so the VAT was £20. Taking 20% off £120 gives the wrong answer (£96), because VAT is charged on the net price, not the gross.

How VAT and GST work around the world

About 175 countries charge a value-added tax. It is called VAT in Europe and most of Africa, GST (goods and services tax) in Australia, Canada, India, New Zealand, Singapore and Malaysia, IVA in Spanish- and Portuguese-speaking countries, and consumption tax in Japan. Businesses charge it on each sale and reclaim the tax they paid on their own purchases, so the final consumer bears the full tax.

  • Standard rate: applies to most goods and services. It ranges from 4.5% in Andorra to 27% in Hungary.
  • Reduced rates: lower rates for essentials such as food, medicine, books, public transport or hotels. Many countries have one or two.
  • Zero rate and exemptions: zero-rated items (exports, often basic food) carry 0% VAT but the seller can still reclaim input tax. Exempt items (usually finance, insurance, health and education) carry no VAT and the seller cannot reclaim it.
  • No VAT: the United States has no national VAT and uses state sales taxes instead; Hong Kong, Kuwait and Qatar have no VAT.

European Union: all 27 member states must charge a standard rate of at least 15%. Hungary is highest at 27%, followed by Croatia, Denmark and Sweden at 25% and Finland at 25.5%; Luxembourg is lowest at 17%. Gulf states: the UAE and Oman charge 5%, Bahrain 10% and Saudi Arabia 15%. Canada combines a 5% federal GST with provincial tax, so the rate depends on the province; each one is listed separately below.

Worked examples

Add 20% UK VAT to £250

£250 × 0.20 = £50 VAT. Total £300.

Remove 19% German VAT from €119

€119 ÷ 1.19 = €100 net. VAT €19.

Add 18% Indian GST to ₹10,000

₹10,000 × 0.18 = ₹1,800 GST (₹900 CGST + ₹900 SGST within one state). Total ₹11,800.

Remove 5% UAE VAT from AED 525

AED 525 ÷ 1.05 = AED 500 net. VAT AED 25.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add VAT to a price?

Multiply the net price by 1 plus the VAT rate. For 20% VAT, £100 × 1.20 = £120, of which £20 is VAT.

How do I remove VAT from a price?

Divide the gross price by 1 plus the VAT rate. For 20% VAT, £120 ÷ 1.20 = £100 net, so the VAT is £20. Do not subtract 20% of the gross price.

Which country has the highest VAT rate?

Hungary, at 27%. In the EU, Croatia, Denmark and Sweden charge 25% and Finland 25.5%.

Which countries have no VAT?

The United States has no national VAT and uses state sales taxes instead. Hong Kong, Kuwait and Qatar also have no VAT.

What is the difference between VAT and GST?

They are the same kind of tax: a tax on consumption collected at each stage of the supply chain, with businesses reclaiming the tax on their purchases. GST is the name used in Australia, Canada, India, New Zealand, Singapore and some other countries.

What are India's GST rates in 2026?

Since 22 September 2025, most goods and services are taxed at 5% or 18%. A 40% rate applies to luxury and sin goods, and gold is taxed at 3%. The 12% and 28% slabs were removed.

What is the VAT rate in the UAE and Saudi Arabia?

The UAE charges 5% VAT. Saudi Arabia charges 15%.

What is the minimum VAT rate in the EU?

EU law requires a standard rate of at least 15%. Member states can apply up to two reduced rates of at least 5% to listed goods and services, and some keep lower legacy rates.
