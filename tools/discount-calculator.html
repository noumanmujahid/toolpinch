What Will You Pay After the Discount?

Calculate the amount saved and final price after a percentage discount.

Discount Calculator

Enter values to see one clear answer.

The short answer

How it works

Multiply the price by the discount and divide by 100 to get the saving, then subtract it: 25% off $80 saves 80 × 25 ÷ 100 = $20, so you pay $60. A faster route is to multiply by what you keep: 25% off means you pay 75%, and $80 × 0.75 = $60.

Worked examples

Simple sale

$120 jacket at 30% off: saving $36, price $84.

Stacked discounts

20% off, then an extra 10% at the till on $100: $100 → $80 → $72. That is 28% off in total, not 30%.

Discount, then sales tax

$60 after discount plus 8% tax: 60 × 1.08 = $64.80. Tax is normally charged on the discounted price.

Find the original price

Paid $45 after 25% off: 45 ÷ 0.75 = $60 original price.

What percentage off was it?

If you know the old and new prices, the discount percentage is (old − new) ÷ old × 100. A price cut from $120 to $90 is 30 ÷ 120 × 100 = 25% off.

Common mistakes

  • Adding stacked discounts. 20% plus 10% is 28% off, because the second discount applies to the already reduced price.
  • Removing a discount by adding the percentage back. $60 plus 25% is $75, not the $80 original. Divide by 0.75 instead.
  • Ignoring shipping and tax. Compare the final amount you pay, not just the headline percentage.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a discount?

Multiply the original price by the discount percentage and divide by 100 to get the saving, then subtract it from the price. 25% off $80 is $20 off, so you pay $60.

How do I calculate two discounts on top of each other?

Apply them one after the other. 20% off then 10% off leaves 0.8 × 0.9 = 72% of the price, which is 28% off in total.

How do I find the original price from a sale price?

Divide the sale price by 1 minus the discount as a decimal. A $45 price after 25% off was 45 ÷ 0.75 = $60.

How do I work out the percentage off?

Subtract the sale price from the original, divide by the original and multiply by 100. $120 down to $90 is 25% off.

Is sales tax charged before or after a discount?

Usually after: tax is charged on the price you actually pay. Manufacturer coupons can be treated differently in some US states.

Is buy one get one half price the same as 50% off?

No. You pay 1.5 prices for 2 items, which is 25% off each item.
