Expense-to-Income Ratio

Measure how much income is already committed.

How it works

Total committed expenses are divided by take-home income. This is a personal cash-flow ratio, not a lender debt-to-income calculation.

Why this number matters

Money is abstract; time is not. This calculator turns an everyday decision into a number you can feel, compare and act on.

Keep the result honest

Use take-home income when possible, enter realistic working time, and treat estimates as a decision aid rather than financial advice.
