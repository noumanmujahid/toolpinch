How Much Surface Area Does This Roof Cover?

Estimate sloped gable roof area from building dimensions and roof pitch.

Roof Area Calculator

Enter values to see one clear answer.

Method

For a symmetrical gable roof, slope factor = √(1 + (rise ÷ 12)²). Roof area = building length × building width × slope factor.

Example

A 12 m by 8 m building with a 6:12 gable pitch has about 107.33 m² of roof surface before waste or overhangs.

Common questions

How do I calculate a sloped roof surface area?

Multiply the building plan area by the slope factor derived from the roof rise per 12 units of run.

Does the roof measurement include overhangs and waste?

No. Add overhang dimensions before calculating and apply a separate material-waste allowance appropriate to the project.
