The short answer

How it works

To complete a proportion A : B = C : ?, cross-multiply: ? = B × C ÷ A. For 3 : 5 = 12 : ?, the answer is 5 × 12 ÷ 3 = 20. To simplify a ratio, divide both sides by their greatest common factor: 18 : 24 ÷ 6 = 3 : 4.

Worked examples

Recipe

2 cups of flour make 12 cookies. For 30 cookies: 2 × 30 ÷ 12 = 5 cups.

Screen size

A 16 : 9 image 1,920 pixels wide is 1,920 × 9 ÷ 16 = 1,080 pixels tall.

Share money

Split $1,000 in the ratio 2 : 3. Total parts 5, one part $200: $400 and $600.

Map scale

At 1 : 50,000, 4 cm on the map is 200,000 cm = 2 km.

Ratios, fractions and percentages

A ratio of 2 : 3 compares two parts. As fractions of the whole, the parts are 2/5 and 3/5, or 40% and 60%. To turn a part-to-part ratio into a share of the total, add the parts and divide each by the sum.

Common mistakes

  • Keep the order. 3 : 5 is not the same as 5 : 3; keep the same quantity first on both sides of a proportion.
  • Use the same units. Convert 30 minutes : 2 hours to 30 : 120 = 1 : 4 before simplifying.
  • Not every relationship is proportional. Doubling a recipe's baking time or a fixed fee does not scale with the ratio.

Frequently asked questions

How do I solve a ratio or proportion?

Cross-multiply. For A : B = C : D, A × D = B × C, so the missing value is D = B × C ÷ A.

How do I simplify a ratio?

Divide each number by their greatest common factor. 18 : 24 has a GCF of 6, so it simplifies to 3 : 4.

How do I split an amount in a ratio?

Add the parts, divide the amount by that total to get one part, then multiply. $1,000 in a 2 : 3 ratio is $400 and $600.

How do I convert a ratio to a percentage?

Divide each part by the sum of the parts and multiply by 100. 2 : 3 is 40% and 60%.

What is the ratio of 1920 by 1080?

16 : 9. Both numbers divide by 120.

Can ratios have decimals?

Yes. You can multiply both sides by the same number to clear decimals: 1.5 : 2 is the same as 3 : 4.
