The short answer

How many federal pay periods are in 2027?

26 pay periods. Leave year 2027 starts on Sunday, January 10, 2027 (pay period 1) and ends on Saturday, January 8, 2028 (pay period 26). The deadline to schedule use-or-lose annual leave is Saturday, November 27, 2027.

Federal employees paid by the USDA National Finance Center (NFC) get 26 paydays in calendar 2027. The first deposit is Monday, January 4 (pay period 25 of 2026) and the last is Monday, December 20 (pay period 24 of 2027). Months with three deposits: March, August.

How federal pay dates work

NFC (USDA and many agencies)

Pay settles on the second Monday after the pay period ends. If that Monday is a federal holiday, it moves to Tuesday. In 2027 that happens four times: after Washington's Birthday, Independence Day, Columbus Day and, for pay period 26, MLK Day 2028.

DFAS, IBC and GSA

Defense (DFAS), Interior Business Center (IBC) and GSA payroll use the same 26 pay periods but pay on a different day of the following week. Your pay period numbers and leave dates are the same; check your earnings statement for your exact pay day.

Pay raises and step increases

Annual pay adjustments take effect on the first day of the first pay period starting on or after January 1, which in 2027 is January 10. The first paycheck that reflects them is pay period 1, deposited around February 1 for NFC.

Sources and limitations

Pay period and leave year dates follow the OPM rule that a leave year begins with the first full biweekly pay period of the calendar year (OPM leave year dates). NFC pay dates follow the NFC pay schedule (NFC pay period calendars). Some agencies and part-time or seasonal schedules differ, so confirm with your payroll office before planning around a specific date.

Frequently asked questions

How many pay periods are in 2027 for federal employees?

26. Leave year 2027 runs from Sunday, January 10, 2027 to Saturday, January 8, 2028, which is 26 biweekly pay periods.

When does pay period 1 of 2027 start?

Sunday, January 10, 2027. It ends Saturday, January 23, and NFC pays it on Monday, February 1 (official pay date Thursday, February 4).

Is 2027 a 27 pay period year?

No. Leave year 2027 has the normal 26 pay periods. NFC-paid employees also receive 26 deposits during calendar year 2027.

What is the use-or-lose deadline for 2027?

Saturday, November 27, 2027. Annual leave above your carryover limit (240 hours for most employees) must be scheduled in writing by then to be restored if it is later cancelled for an approved reason.

When is the first federal payday in 2027?

For NFC-paid employees, Monday, January 4, 2027, which pays pay period 25 of 2026. The next is Tuesday, January 19, moved a day by MLK Day.

When does the 2027 leave year end?

Saturday, January 8, 2028, the last day of pay period 26. Leave year 2028 starts Sunday, January 9, 2028.

How much annual leave will I earn in 2027?

104 hours with less than 3 years of service, 160 hours with 3 to 15 years, and 208 hours with 15 years or more, plus 104 hours of sick leave for full-time employees.

Do holidays change federal pay dates?

For NFC, if the Monday settlement date is a federal holiday the deposit moves to Tuesday. In 2027 that happens on February 16, July 6 and October 12, and on January 18, 2028 for pay period 26.
