Birthday Countdown

Enter a date of birth to begin.

Understand the result

How to use this tool

Calculate calendar days and Monday-to-Friday weekdays until the next occurrence of a birthday.

Formula or method

The month and day are placed in the current year, or the next year when that date has passed.

Worked examples

Example 1

A birthday tomorrow returns one calendar day.

Example 2

A birthday across a weekend may have fewer weekdays than calendar days.

Frequently asked questions

What does the birthday countdown calculate?

Calculate calendar days and Monday-to-Friday weekdays until the next occurrence of a birthday.

How is the result calculated?

The month and day are placed in the current year, or the next year when that date has passed.

Are my inputs uploaded?

No. This calculation runs in the current browser and does not require an account.

What should I check before using the result?

Public holidays are not removed from the weekday count.

Limitations

Public holidays are not removed from the weekday count.
