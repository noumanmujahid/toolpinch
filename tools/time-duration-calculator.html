Understand the result

How to use this tool

Find the exact duration between two clock times, including a shift or event that crosses midnight.

Formula or method

Both times are converted to minutes. When the end is earlier than the start, 24 hours are added to the end.

Worked examples

Example 1

9:15 to 17:45 equals 8 hours 30 minutes.

Example 2

22:00 to 06:00 equals 8 hours across midnight.

Frequently asked questions

What does the time duration calculator calculate?

Find the exact duration between two clock times, including a shift or event that crosses midnight.

How is the result calculated?

Both times are converted to minutes. When the end is earlier than the start, 24 hours are added to the end.

Are my inputs uploaded?

No. This calculation runs in the current browser and does not require an account.

What should I check before using the result?

The tool calculates one continuous duration and does not subtract breaks.

Limitations

The tool calculates one continuous duration and does not subtract breaks.
