The short answer

How it works

Multiply the bill by the tip percentage, add it to the bill, then divide by the number of people. An $84 bill with an 18% tip is 84 × 0.18 = $15.12 tip, $99.12 in total, or $24.78 each for four people.

Uneven split

One person ordered $40 of a $100 bill. With 20% tip they pay 40 × 1.2 = $48; the rest share $72.

Tipping customs differ by country

In the United States, 15% to 20% is usual for table service, and many people tip 20% or more. In Canada 15% to 20% is also common. In the UK and much of Europe a 10% to 12.5% service charge is often already added to the bill, so check before tipping again. In Japan and South Korea tipping is not expected. In Australia and New Zealand, tipping is optional and usually reserved for good service.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a tip?

Multiply the bill by the tip percentage as a decimal. An 18% tip on $84 is 84 × 0.18 = $15.12.

How do I split a bill with tip?

Add the tip to the bill and divide the total by the number of people. $99.12 shared by four is $24.78 each.

Should I tip on the total before or after tax?

Etiquette guides usually suggest tipping on the pre-tax amount, though many people tip on the total for simplicity. The difference is small.

How much should I tip at a restaurant in the US?

15% to 20% of the pre-tax bill is standard for table service, with 20% or more common for good service.

What is a quick way to work out a 15% tip?

Take 10% (move the decimal one place left) and add half of it. For $60: $6 + $3 = $9.

What if one person ordered much more?

Work out each person's share of the food, then add the same tip percentage to each share instead of splitting equally.
