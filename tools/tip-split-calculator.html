How Much Should Each Person Pay?

Add a tip and split the complete bill evenly.

Tip and Split Calculator

Enter values to see one clear answer.

Method

Tip = bill × tip percentage ÷ 100. Add the tip to the bill, then divide the total by the number of people.

Example

An $80 bill with 18% tip totals $94.40, or $23.60 each for four people.
