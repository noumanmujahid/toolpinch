How Far Apart Are These Two Points?

Find coordinate distance and the midpoint between two points.

Distance and Midpoint Calculator

Enter values to see one clear answer.

Method

Distance = √((x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²). Midpoint = ((x₁ + x₂) ÷ 2, (y₁ + y₂) ÷ 2).

Example

Points (1,2) and (7,10) are 10 units apart and have midpoint (4,6).
