How to use this tool

Compare an Earth duration with one selected planet using approximate solar-day lengths and orbital periods.

Formula or method

Earth time is converted to Earth days, then divided by the selected planet’s solar day and orbital period.

Worked examples

Example 1

One Earth year is about 0.53 Mars years.

Example 2

Frequently asked questions

What does the planet time converter calculate?

Compare an Earth duration with one selected planet using approximate solar-day lengths and orbital periods.

How is the result calculated?

Earth time is converted to Earth days, then divided by the selected planet’s solar day and orbital period.

Are my inputs uploaded?

No. This calculation runs in the current browser and does not require an account.

What should I check before using the result?

Values are educational approximations and do not describe biological aging.
