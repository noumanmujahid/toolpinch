Loan Amortization Calculator

Method

For a fixed-rate amortized loan, payment = P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ ÷ ((1 + r)ⁿ − 1), where r is the rate per payment period and n is the number of payments.

Example

A $200,000 fixed loan at 6% for 30 years has a monthly principal-and-interest payment of about $1,199.10.

Common questions

What does an amortization schedule show?

It separates each fixed payment into principal and interest and shows the remaining loan balance.

Can I calculate monthly, weekly or annual loan payments?

Yes. Choose monthly, biweekly, weekly or annual payments; the calculator adjusts the periodic interest rate and payment count.

Does this calculator include APR fees?

No. It uses the entered nominal annual interest rate and does not add lender fees, insurance, taxes or a variable APR.
