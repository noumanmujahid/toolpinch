The short answer

How it works

To add or subtract fractions, rewrite them over a common denominator: 1/2 + 1/3 = 3/6 + 2/6 = 5/6. To multiply, multiply tops and bottoms: 2/3 × 9/10 = 18/30 = 3/5. To divide, multiply by the flipped second fraction: 3/4 ÷ 1/8 = 3/4 × 8/1 = 6.

Worked examples

Subtract

3/4 − 1/6. Common denominator 12: 9/12 − 2/12 = 7/12.

Mixed numbers

2 1/2 + 1 3/4 = 5/2 + 7/4 = 10/4 + 7/4 = 17/4 = 4 1/4.

Recipe scaling

A recipe needs 2/3 cup of milk; make 1 1/2 batches: 2/3 × 3/2 = 1 cup.

Simplify

24/36: the greatest common factor is 12, so 24/36 = 2/3.

Common mistakes

  • Adding denominators. 1/2 + 1/3 is not 2/5. The denominators must match before you add numerators.
  • Forgetting to flip when dividing. Dividing by 1/8 is the same as multiplying by 8.
  • Mixed numbers in multiplication. Convert 2 1/2 to 5/2 first; multiplying the whole parts and fraction parts separately gives the wrong answer.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add fractions with different denominators?

Find a common denominator (the least common multiple of the denominators), convert each fraction, then add the numerators. 1/2 + 1/3 = 3/6 + 2/6 = 5/6.

How do I divide fractions?

Multiply the first fraction by the reciprocal of the second. 3/4 ÷ 1/8 = 3/4 × 8 = 6.

How do I simplify a fraction?

Divide the numerator and denominator by their greatest common factor. 24/36 ÷ 12/12 = 2/3.

How do I convert a mixed number to an improper fraction?

Multiply the whole number by the denominator and add the numerator. 2 1/2 = (2 × 2 + 1)/2 = 5/2.

How do I turn a fraction into a decimal?

Divide the numerator by the denominator. 3/8 = 3 ÷ 8 = 0.375.

Why can't the denominator be zero?

Division by zero is undefined, so a fraction with a zero denominator has no value.
