How to use the work hours calculator

Use this calculator to check a normal or overnight shift, remove an unpaid lunch, and estimate a repeated weekly schedule. It returns standard hours and minutes rather than hiding the break deduction.

Formula

Paid shift minutes = end time − start time − unpaid break. Weekly hours = paid shift hours × workdays.

Worked examples

9:00 AM to 5:30 PM with lunch

The shift lasts 8 hours 30 minutes. Subtracting a 30-minute unpaid lunch leaves 8 paid hours.

Five identical workdays

Five 8-hour paid shifts equal 40 paid hours per week.

Overnight shift

A 10:00 PM start and 6:30 AM end is treated as crossing midnight. With a 30-minute break, paid time is 8 hours.

Frequently asked questions

Are paid breaks deducted?

No. Enter only unpaid break minutes.

Can it calculate overnight work?

Yes. An end time earlier than the start is treated as the following day.

Does it calculate wages?

No. Use the overtime or pay calculators after finding paid hours.

Does it subtract holidays?

No. It calculates the shift pattern entered.

Assumptions and limitations

Results depend on the values entered and are educational estimates. Pay figures exclude taxes, deductions and benefits. Workplace rules, rounding practices and legal requirements vary by location and employer.
