What crypto market cap means

Market cap is a simple estimate calculated by multiplying price by circulating supply. It is often used to compare relative asset size, but it does not measure safety or future performance.

Use carefully

Crypto supply data can vary by source. Always check the original project documentation and independent data sources before relying on a market cap number.

Safe crypto calculator rules

  • Use your own verified price, fee and supply numbers.
