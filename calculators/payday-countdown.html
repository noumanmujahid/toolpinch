Read our calculation and review methodology.

Does the countdown remove holidays?

It counts calendar days and Monday-to-Friday weekdays. Country-specific public holidays are not removed.

What should I enter?

Use take-home income and realistic working time whenever possible. The result is an educational estimate and not financial, tax, employment or investment advice.
