What is the real annual cost of commuting?

Price fuel, fares and the working time lost to commuting. Time cost uses your take-home hourly pay. You can interpret the full value or mentally discount it based on how useful your commute feels.

Worked example

A 60-minute daily commute across 240 workdays uses 240 hours per year before fuel, fares, parking and vehicle costs are added.

Should commuting time count as a cost?
