Understand your result

How many hours do I need to work to afford something?

Turn any price into hours and days of your working life. Required hours equal price divided by take-home hourly pay. Workdays equal those hours divided by your workday length.

Worked example

At $25 take-home per hour, a $1,000 purchase costs 40 work hours, or five eight-hour workdays.

How the calculation works

Read our calculation and review methodology.

Why calculate a price in work hours?

Work hours make the tradeoff tangible and help you compare a purchase with the time required to earn its price.

What should I enter?

Use take-home income and realistic working time whenever possible. The result is an educational estimate and not financial, tax, employment or investment advice.
