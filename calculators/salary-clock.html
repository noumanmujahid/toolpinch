Understand your result

How much money do I earn per hour?

Watch your earnings grow second by second during a shift. Annual pay is divided by your stated workdays and hours, then by 3,600 for a per-second rate.

Worked example

With $60,000 annual take-home pay, 260 workdays and eight paid hours per day, the effective rate is about $28.85 per hour, $230.77 per workday and $0.0080 per second.

How the calculation works

Annual pay is divided by your stated workdays and hours, then by 3,600 for a per-second rate.

Read our calculation and review methodology.

Should I use gross or take-home salary?

Use take-home pay when you want the result to reflect money you can actually spend. Gross salary is more useful for comparing compensation.

What should I enter?

Use take-home income and realistic working time whenever possible. The result is an educational estimate and not financial, tax, employment or investment advice.
