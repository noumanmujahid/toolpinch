What percentage of my salary goes to expenses?

See what percentage of take-home salary is already committed to expenses. Total committed expenses are divided by take-home income. This is a personal cash-flow ratio, not a lender debt-to-income calculation.

Worked example

If fixed monthly expenses total $2,650 against $4,000 take-home pay, 66.3% of income is already committed.

How the calculation works

Total committed expenses are divided by take-home income. This is a personal cash-flow ratio, not a lender debt-to-income calculation.

Read our calculation and review methodology.

Is this the same as lender debt-to-income?

No. This tool measures committed expenses against take-home pay; lender calculations usually compare qualifying debt with gross income.

What should I enter?

Use take-home income and realistic working time whenever possible. The result is an educational estimate and not financial, tax, employment or investment advice.
