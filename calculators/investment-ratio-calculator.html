How much of my salary am I investing?

Measure how much of your salary goes toward long-term investing. Monthly investing is divided by take-home salary. The projection shows contributions only, so market performance is not implied.

Worked example

Investing $500 from $4,000 monthly take-home pay means 12.5% of salary is being invested.

Does the projection estimate market returns?

No. It shows principal contributions only, avoiding an unsupported promise about future performance.

What should I enter?

Use take-home income and realistic working time whenever possible. The result is an educational estimate and not financial, tax, employment or investment advice.
