Understand your result

How does my budget compare with the 50/30/20 rule?

Compare your salary with a needs, wants and future-money framework. The reference assigns 50% to needs, 30% to wants and 20% to savings, investing or extra debt. Your circumstances may require a different split.

Worked example

On $4,000 take-home pay, the reference amounts are $2,000 for needs, $1,200 for wants and $800 for saving, investing or extra debt payments.

How the calculation works

Must every budget follow 50/30/20?

No. It is a reference framework. High housing costs, debt or irregular income may require a different allocation.

What should I enter?

Use take-home income and realistic working time whenever possible. The result is an educational estimate and not financial, tax, employment or investment advice.
