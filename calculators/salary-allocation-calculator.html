Understand your result

How should I divide my monthly salary?

Divide take-home pay across needs, wants, debt, savings and investing. Each category is divided by monthly take-home income. The result also compares your future-money contribution with a flexible 20% reference point.

Worked example

From $4,000 monthly take-home pay, $1,900 of needs equals 47.5%, while $500 saved and $500 invested create a 25% future-money rate.

Is there one correct salary allocation?

No. Housing costs, family responsibilities, debt and location differ. Use the percentages as visibility, not a universal rule.

What should I enter?

Use take-home income and realistic working time whenever possible. The result is an educational estimate and not financial, tax, employment or investment advice.
