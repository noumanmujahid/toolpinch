How much does a monthly subscription cost over time?

Reveal what a monthly subscription costs in money and work time. The calculation assumes the price stays unchanged. It does not include inflation, discounts or investment returns.

Worked example

A $20 monthly subscription kept for 10 years costs $2,400. At $25 take-home per hour, that represents 96 hours of work.

How the calculation works

Does the result include price increases?

The estimate assumes a constant monthly price, so future increases, discounts and investment returns are excluded.

What should I enter?

Use take-home income and realistic working time whenever possible. The result is an educational estimate and not financial, tax, employment or investment advice.
