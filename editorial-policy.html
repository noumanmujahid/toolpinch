Editorial standards

Editorial Policy

How ToolPinch writes, calculates, reviews and corrects calculator content.

Purpose and scope

Editorial content explains work hours, pay conversions, dates and time in plain language. Every priority calculator should state its formula, provide realistic examples and identify meaningful limitations.

Sources and rules

General arithmetic is derived transparently from user inputs. Jurisdiction-specific employment claims must be supported by current official sources. ToolPinch does not invent search volume, legal eligibility or guaranteed financial outcomes.

Examples and assumptions

Worked examples use illustrative numbers, not personal recommendations. Pay examples are gross unless clearly labelled otherwise. Date tools distinguish calendar days from weekdays and identify whether holidays are excluded.

Corrections

Material formula or factual errors are corrected in the generator and rebuilt across the site. Visitors can report issues through the contact page.

Commercial independence

Advertising or affiliate relationships, if introduced, will not change calculator formulas. Sponsored placements will be labelled and will not be presented as independent calculations.
