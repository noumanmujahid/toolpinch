Editorial Policy

ToolPinch pages are written to explain a calculation clearly and honestly.

Standards

Each calculator page states its formula, gives worked examples, and lists assumptions and limits. Pay results are labeled as gross estimates before taxes and deductions.

Accuracy

Formulas are checked against hand calculations before publishing. ToolPinch avoids invented statistics, fake search volumes and claims that a tool provides legal or payroll certainty.

Corrections

Report an error through the contact page. Confirmed errors are corrected and the page is updated.

Independence

Any future advertising or affiliate links will be disclosed and will not change how results are calculated.
