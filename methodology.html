Methodology

This page summarizes how ToolPinch calculators work.

Time and hours

Times are converted to minutes. When an end time is earlier than its start, the shift is treated as ending the next day. Paid time = shift length minus unpaid breaks.

Pay

Hourly pay from salary = annual salary ÷ (hours per week × working weeks). Paycheck amounts use 52 weekly, 26 biweekly, 24 semimonthly and 12 monthly periods per year. Overtime pay = overtime hours × rate × multiplier.

Dates

Calendar days include every date. Weekday counts cover Monday to Friday and do not remove public holidays. Time zone conversion uses the browser IANA time zone data for the chosen date.

Limits

Results are gross estimates. They exclude taxes, benefits, deductions, rounding rules and local labor law. Confirm important figures with the relevant authority.
