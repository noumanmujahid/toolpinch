Privacy Policy

ToolPinch offers free calculators for work hours, pay, dates and time. This page explains what is processed and what is not.

Calculator information

Work-hour, salary, date and time values are calculated in your browser. ToolPinch does not require an account and does not send calculator inputs to a server or to any AI provider.

Text helper tools

The SEO and text helpers on the site also run in your browser. No AI service is connected to any page.

Analytics and logs

The site is hosted on Vercel and may use Vercel Web Analytics or Speed Insights for aggregate visit and performance data. Hosting infrastructure may process standard request data such as IP address, browser type, referring page and requested URL for security and delivery.

Cookies and advertising

ToolPinch does not use advertising cookies today. If advertising or consent tools are added, this policy will be updated before they go live, with the relevant partners and choices.

External links

Third-party sites have their own privacy practices, which ToolPinch does not control.

Contact

Privacy questions: toolpinchofficial@gmail.com. Please do not send confidential payroll records or personal identifiers.
