Privacy Policy

This policy explains what ToolPinch processes, what it does not collect intentionally, and which infrastructure services help deliver the site.

Calculator information

Work-hour, salary, date and time values are calculated in your browser. ToolPinch does not require an account and does not intentionally send calculator inputs to ToolPinch or an AI provider.

Analytics and technical logs

ToolPinch uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors find and use the site, such as which pages are visited, which calculators are used and which files are downloaded. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as your approximate location, device and browser. ToolPinch does not send calculator inputs, names or other personal details to Google Analytics. You can learn how Google uses this data at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

The site is hosted on Vercel and may also use Vercel Web Analytics. Hosting infrastructure may process standard request information such as IP address, browser, referring page and requested URL for security, delivery and diagnostics.

Cookies and local storage

The calculators themselves do not need cookies. Some features may store interface state, such as a selected option, in your browser. Advertising partners described below may set cookies or use similar technologies when ads are shown.

Advertising

ToolPinch shows, or may show, ads provided by Google AdSense to keep the calculators free.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.
  • Other third-party vendors or ad networks may also serve ads on ToolPinch. You can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland will be asked for consent before personalized ads or non-essential cookies are used.

External links

Links to third-party websites are governed by those sites’ privacy policies. ToolPinch does not control their collection or retention practices.

Data choices and contact

Do not enter confidential payroll records or personal identifiers into a public website. Privacy questions can be sent to toolpinchofficial@gmail.com.

Policy changes

Material changes will be reflected on this page. Effective date: October 30, 2026.
