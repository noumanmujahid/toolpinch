Privacy

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what ToolPinch processes, what it does not collect intentionally, and which infrastructure services help deliver the site.

Calculator information

Work-hour, salary, date and time values are calculated in your browser. ToolPinch does not require an account and does not intentionally send calculator inputs to ToolPinch or an AI provider.

Analytics and technical logs

ToolPinch uses Vercel hosting and may use Vercel Web Analytics or Speed Insights to understand aggregate visits and technical performance. Hosting infrastructure may process standard request information such as IP address, browser, referring page and requested URL for security, delivery and diagnostics.

Cookies and local storage

The calculators do not require advertising cookies. Some browser features may temporarily store interface state on your device. Future advertising or consent features will require this policy to be updated before activation.

External links

Links to third-party websites are governed by those sites’ privacy policies. ToolPinch does not control their collection or retention practices.

Data choices and contact

Do not enter confidential payroll records or personal identifiers into a public website. Privacy questions can be sent to toolpinchofficial@gmail.com.

Policy changes

Material changes will be reflected on this page. Effective date: September 29, 2026.
