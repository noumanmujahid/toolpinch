Choose the right date or time tool

Use Date Difference for calendar spans, Time Duration for clock times, Timezone Converter for the same moment in another region, and Countdown or Stopwatch for a running timer. Birthday Countdown answers a recurring calendar-date question.

Calendar days and weekdays answer different questions

Calendar days include every date. Weekdays count Monday through Friday and do not automatically remove country-specific public holidays. Check the result type before using it for a deadline.

Time zones and daylight saving

A fixed hour offset is not reliable year-round in regions that observe daylight saving time. The timezone converter uses the browser's current IANA timezone information for the selected date.

Overnight durations

When an end time is earlier than its start time, the duration calculator treats the end as occurring on the following day. This supports overnight shifts and travel schedules.

Clear estimates, not hidden assumptions

ToolPinch shows the inputs and method used for each answer. Results depend on the dates, times or pay values entered and may require confirmation against workplace policies or official rules.
