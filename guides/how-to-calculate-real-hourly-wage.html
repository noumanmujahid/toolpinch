How to Calculate Your Real Hourly Wage

A practical explanation with a clear formula, realistic example and a calculator you can use privately.

By ToolPinch Editorial · Reviewed September 29, 2026 · 5 minute read

Real hourly wage formula

Divide annual take-home pay by annual work-related hours. For a basic estimate, annual hours equal workdays per year multiplied by paid hours per day.

Example using a $60,000 take-home salary

With 260 workdays and eight paid hours per day, annual working time is 2,080 hours and the effective rate is about $28.85 per hour.

Include unpaid working time when relevant

Commuting, required preparation and unpaid administration can be added to annual hours when they are unavoidable parts of earning the income.

Gross wage and real wage answer different questions

Gross wage helps compare job offers. Take-home hourly wage is usually more useful when deciding whether you can afford a purchase or recurring expense.
