How Many Work Hours Does a Purchase Cost?

A practical explanation with a clear formula, realistic example and a calculator you can use privately.

By ToolPinch Editorial · Reviewed September 29, 2026 · 5 minute read

The work-hours formula

Divide the full purchase price by your take-home hourly wage. Divide that answer by your usual daily working hours to express the cost in workdays.

A simple purchase example

A $1,000 purchase at $25 take-home per hour requires 40 hours of work, equal to five eight-hour workdays.

Use the complete purchase price

Include sales tax, delivery, financing costs and required accessories when they materially change what you will pay.

Compare the result with expected use

Work hours show the earning sacrifice. Expected years of use, maintenance and resale value provide the other side of the decision.
