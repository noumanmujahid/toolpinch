How to Calculate the Real Cost of a Commute

A practical explanation with a clear formula, realistic example and a calculator you can use privately.

By ToolPinch Editorial · Reviewed September 29, 2026 · 5 minute read

Count annual commuting time

Multiply round-trip minutes by commuting days per week and working weeks per year, then divide by 60 to get annual hours.

Add direct commuting expenses

Include public transport, fuel, tolls and parking. Vehicle wear and insurance may be included when comparing remote and office work.

Put a value on the time

Multiplying commuting hours by take-home hourly pay gives a useful opportunity-cost estimate, but the tool keeps cash and time visible separately.

Example of a one-hour daily commute

One hour across 240 office days consumes 240 hours a year, which equals thirty eight-hour days before any travel spending is included.
