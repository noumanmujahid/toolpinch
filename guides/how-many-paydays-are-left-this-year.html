Money and time guide

How Many Paydays Are Left This Year?

A practical way to turn an abstract money question into a decision you can understand.

Start with take-home pay

Gross salary is useful for comparing jobs, but take-home pay is usually better for personal decisions because it reflects what reaches you.

Convert money into time

Divide the cost by your effective hourly pay. Then divide those hours by your normal workday to make the result easier to feel.

Add the hidden costs

Commuting, unpaid administration, fees and recurring charges can change the result. Include them when they are relevant.

Use the number as a prompt

A calculator cannot decide what brings value to your life. It can make the tradeoff visible before you decide.
