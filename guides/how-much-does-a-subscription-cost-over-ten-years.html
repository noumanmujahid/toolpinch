How Much Does a Subscription Cost Over 10 Years?

A practical explanation with a clear formula, realistic example and a calculator you can use privately.

By ToolPinch Editorial · Reviewed September 29, 2026 · 5 minute read

Calculate the long-term cash cost

Multiply the monthly price by 12 and then by the number of years. A $20 subscription kept for 10 years costs $2,400.

Translate the total into work time

At $25 take-home per hour, that $2,400 total represents 96 hours of work, or twelve eight-hour workdays.

Account for changing prices carefully

A basic calculator assumes the price stays constant. Real subscriptions may increase, offer annual discounts or be cancelled before the selected period ends.

Review subscriptions by value, not price alone

A subscription used daily may offer better value than a cheaper service rarely opened. Long-term cost helps make that comparison deliberate.
