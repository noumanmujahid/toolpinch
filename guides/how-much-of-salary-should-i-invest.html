How Much of My Salary Should I Invest?

A practical explanation with a clear formula, realistic example and a calculator you can use privately.

By ToolPinch Editorial · Reviewed September 29, 2026 · 5 minute read

Calculate your investment ratio

Divide the amount invested each month by monthly take-home salary and multiply by 100. Investing $500 from $4,000 equals 12.5%.

Keep saving and investing distinct

Emergency cash and short-term savings serve different purposes from investments that can change in value and are intended for longer horizons.

Do not treat projections as promises

Future returns are uncertain. ToolPinch shows the principal you contribute without presenting an assumed market return as a guaranteed outcome.

Consider obligations before increasing the ratio

Essential expenses, emergency reserves, expensive debt, taxes and access to employer benefits can all affect an appropriate contribution level.
