About ToolPinch

ToolPinch creates clear browser-based calculators for work hours, pay, dates and time.

What ToolPinch does

ToolPinch helps people calculate paid hours, subtract unpaid breaks, compare salary and pay periods, estimate overtime, count dates and convert time. A smaller collection of everyday and SEO helpers remains available as secondary tools.

How the tools are built

Calculator inputs are processed in the browser whenever possible. Important formulas, examples, assumptions and limitations are written on the tool page so results can be checked rather than accepted as a black box.

Who maintains the site

ToolPinch is independently maintained by the ToolPinch editorial team. Questions, corrections and bug reports can be sent through the contact page.

What ToolPinch is not

ToolPinch is not payroll software, an employer, a labor authority, or a financial, legal or tax adviser. Pay results are educational estimates and should be checked against current workplace rules.
