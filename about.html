About ToolPinch

ToolPinch builds clear browser-based calculators for work hours, pay, dates and time.

What ToolPinch does

The tools help people total paid hours, subtract unpaid breaks, convert salary and pay periods, estimate overtime, count dates and convert time. A smaller set of everyday and SEO helpers is available as secondary tools.

How the tools are built

Calculations run in your browser. Money and time pages state their formulas, assumptions and limits so results can be checked. See the methodology and editorial policy.

Who maintains the site

ToolPinch is independently maintained. Questions, corrections and bug reports are welcome through the contact page.

What ToolPinch is not

ToolPinch is not payroll software, an employer, a labor authority, or a financial, legal or tax adviser. Pay results are educational estimates.
