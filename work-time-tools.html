Choose the right work-time calculator

Use Break Deduction for one shift, Work Hours for a repeated schedule, and Timesheet when several shifts have different start times or breaks. Once paid time is known, Overtime and Salary to Hourly can translate it into earnings.

Build the calculation in a reliable order

Start with clock times, subtract only unpaid breaks, total the paid hours, and then apply the confirmed regular or overtime rate. This sequence makes mistakes easier to spot.

Hours and decimal hours

Standard time shows hours and minutes, while many payroll systems use decimal hours. Thirty minutes equals 0.50 hours, but 30 minutes is not 0.30 hours.

Clear estimates, not hidden assumptions

ToolPinch shows the inputs and method used for each answer. Results depend on the dates, times or pay values entered and may require confirmation against workplace policies or official rules.
