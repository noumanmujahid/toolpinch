Choose the calculation you need

Start with the question you are trying to answer. Use Work Hours or Break Deduction for one shift, Timesheet for several shifts, Salary to Hourly for an annual offer, and Pay Period Converter when two jobs quote pay on different schedules.

How these calculators work together

First calculate paid time after unpaid breaks. Total multiple shifts when necessary. Then enter confirmed regular and overtime hours into the pay calculator. Keeping time and pay as separate steps makes each result easier to inspect and correct.

Biweekly and semimonthly pay are different

Biweekly schedules normally create 26 pay periods per year. Semimonthly schedules create 24. The Pay Period Converter keeps those schedules separate and uses annual pay as a common baseline.

Clear estimates, not hidden assumptions

ToolPinch shows the inputs and method used for each answer. Pay results are gross estimates before taxes, deductions, benefits and jurisdiction-specific employment rules. Confirm overtime eligibility and payroll rounding with the relevant employer or labor authority.
