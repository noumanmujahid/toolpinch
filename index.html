The idea

Prices hide the sacrifice. Time reveals it.

A $1,000 purchase is not merely $1,000. At $25 take-home per hour, it is 40 hours of your life. A $20 subscription kept for ten years is $2,400. A one-hour commute can consume six working weeks every year.

ToolPinch makes those tradeoffs visible without collecting your financial data.
