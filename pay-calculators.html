Choose the pay calculation you need

Use Salary to Hourly for an annual offer, Pay Period Converter when jobs quote different schedules, and Overtime Pay after confirming which hours qualify for a premium rate. Work Hours, Timesheet and Break Deduction help establish the paid time first.

How the calculators work together

Calculate paid time after unpaid breaks, total multiple shifts, then apply regular and overtime rates. Keeping time and pay as separate steps makes the result easier to inspect.

Biweekly and semimonthly are different

Biweekly schedules normally create 26 payments per year. Semimonthly schedules create 24. The converter uses annual pay as a common baseline.

Clear estimates, not hidden assumptions

ToolPinch shows the inputs and method used for each answer. Results depend on the dates, times or pay values entered and may require confirmation against workplace policies or official rules.
