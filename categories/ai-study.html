AI Study Tools

Generate practice quizzes, flashcards, worksheets, study notes, MCQs, lesson plans, revision plans and speaking prompts from one topic. This category includes 10 focused AI study tools.

Study tools built for practice, not shortcuts

ToolPinch AI Study Tools help students, teachers, tutors and parents create learning drafts quickly. Use them to plan revision, prepare classroom material, build practice questions and explain topics in simpler language.

These tools avoid cheating-focused wording and do not guarantee marks, grades or official answers. Every result should be checked by a learner, teacher, parent or tutor before use.

Best use cases

  • Create a quick quiz from a chapter or topic.
  • Turn notes into flashcards for revision.
  • Draft a worksheet with an answer key.
  • Plan a lesson, exam revision schedule or study timetable.
  • Practice vocabulary and public speaking prompts.
Privacy note: Do not paste private student records, personal data, passwords, medical details or confidential school material into AI tools.
